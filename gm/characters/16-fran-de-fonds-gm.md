# GM FILE — FRAN DE FONDS
## Family Office / Shareholder

**Player-facing file:** `characters/16-fran-de-fonds.md`  
**Core function:** distressed-capital side drama / succession vote / Cal-Arby deal engine  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Fran’s family office is under serious financial pressure.

Cal Lateral arranged/approved lending that helped keep the situation afloat.

Fran needs a meaningful financing/restructuring solution.

Fran has no murder involvement.

The role exists to create:
- a live financing problem;
- vote leverage;
- natural conversations with Cal and Arby;
- optional later chaos if desired.

No bespoke financing mini-game is required.

---

## WHAT FRAN GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Fran knows:
- the family office is materially overextended;
- Cal knows the exposure;
- Cal’s lending is important to the current position;
- Arby may be a viable restructuring / rescue-capital counterparty;
- Fran’s vote has value;
- Penny is an easy social connection through the family network.

---

## WHAT FRAN DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Fran/Cal lending material remains a compact side file.

Final evidence IDs are **TBD** until the master evidence registry is locked.

- substantial exposure;

- pressure on Fran;
without creating a second major investigation tree.

---

## POWER — FAMILY OFFICE ACCESS

Once per game, Fran chooses one currently available exposure directly involving Fran / the family office.

The GM gives the prepared **private exposure / ownership context note**.

This may clarify:
- collateral;
- ownership;
- maturity/pressure;
- who is economically exposed;
- whether a proposed restructuring actually solves the relevant problem.

It may NOT:
- create new assets or debts;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- invent valuations;
- reveal murder facts.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Secure at least £500k of financing/relief/restructuring
This can be achieved through ordinary:
- money;
- signed Deal Slips;
- debt relief;
- refinancing commitment;
- restructuring agreement.

Do not build a tender system.

### 2. Keep distress private until the CEO vote
Success means the true scale of Fran’s problem is not broadly established before the ballot.

Private disclosure to:
- Cal;
- Arby;
- one negotiating counterparty
does not automatically count as failure.

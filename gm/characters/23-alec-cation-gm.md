- represent the employee / organisational consequences of Vantage’s crisis;
- pressure CEO candidates to make public commitments;
- give the succession debate a dimension beyond money and control.

## POWER — EMPLOYEE QUESTION

This replaces the older full hustings mechanic.

### Timing
Use after CEO nominations are set and before the first ballot.

### Procedure
Alec asks **one shared public question** to all nominated candidates.

The question should concern:

- employees;
- organisational change;
- governance;
- leadership accountability;
- workforce impact.

Each candidate gives a short answer.

Keep answers brief enough that the whole beat lasts only a few minutes.

### Limits
The question cannot:

- force disclosure of a private secret;
- demand unreleased evidence;
- override murder mechanics;
- reveal actual votes;
- become a second debate system.

The power is social/public, not evidentiary.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Get employee impact into the succession debate
Success means Alec raises the issue publicly before the CEO vote and candidates have to engage with it.

Using **Employee Question** is the cleanest route.

### 2. Secure two concrete commitments
Alec needs two meaningful commitments from candidates.

For strong success:
- at least one commitment comes from the eventual CEO.

Examples:
- worker consultation before restructuring;
- protected escalation channels;
- board-level workforce reporting;
- transparent redundancy/redeployment process;
- employee representation in governance;
- limits on unilateral reorganisation.

Do not require a bespoke contract mechanic.

A signed Deal Slip or clear written promise is enough.

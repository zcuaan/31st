# GM FILE — POLLY TICIAN
## Government Affairs / Lobbying

**Player-facing file:** `characters/03-polly-tician.md`  
**Core function:** independent failed killer / Act I poison solution / political-access scandal  
**Murder involvement:** ATTEMPTED MURDER, BUT NOT THE FATAL KILLER

---

## CORE TRUTH

Polly Tician deliberately tried to kill Julian Vane.

She added fictional **Aster-9** to Julian’s champagne at approximately 16:40.

Julian drank at approximately 16:45.

By approximately 16:50 he appeared distracted / unsteady.

Polly intended Julian to die.

She later sees that Julian is dead and initially believes there is a serious chance she succeeded.

She did **not** cause Julian’s actual death.

Aster-9 is later established as **nonfatal in the amount/process relevant to the fictional case**.

The fatal mechanism is the counterfeit Neraval arranged by Cole Control and physically swapped by Rita Distribution.

Critical distinction:

> **Polly attempted murder. Polly is not the person who actually caused Julian’s death.**

Do not soften her intent simply because her attempt failed.

---

## THEMATIC FUNCTION

Polly gives the game its first major false solution.

Players should be able to build a very plausible early theory:

- suspicious behaviour near champagne;
- Aster-9 present;
- Julian becomes unsteady;
- Julian later dies.

That theory should be evidence-based and wrong only because it is incomplete.

The first forensic reversal then establishes:

> Aster-9 was present, but it was not the fatal mechanism.

This teaches players early that:

- suspicious conduct can be real;
- guilt for one act is not proof of the final homicide;
- later evidence can change the causal story without making earlier evidence fake.

---

## POLITICAL MOTIVE

Polly’s motive is not random personal hatred.

She believes Julian increasingly used political relationships, access and institutional leverage in damaging ways.

Relevant private concerns include:

- aggressive use of lobbying/political access;
- commercial interests presented through supposedly neutral policy/networking spaces;
- pressure placed on intermediaries and contacts;
- records that could expose Polly’s own role in arranging or presenting access.

Polly’s political-access file is intentionally compromising.

It should create:
- motive;
- reputational risk;
- a separate reason to hide documents;
- conversations with Perry / Lucy / other influence-world characters.

It should **not** create a second evidence tree large enough to compete with the murder.

## POWER — POLITICAL CONTACT

Once per game, Polly chooses one **currently available** political/public-affairs item, organisation, event or named political actor that is referenced in released evidence.

The GM gives the prepared **public/political context note**.

The result may clarify:

- who publicly controls or sponsors an event/organisation;
- whether an introduction was officially independent or commercially connected;
- public relationships between fictional political actors and Vantage;
- relevant public chronology or reputation context.

The power may NOT:

- unlock unreleased evidence;
- reveal private murder facts;
- identify the killer directly;
- create a new political scandal;
- use real-world politicians/parties as fictional participants.

If no prepared result exists, tell Polly before consuming the power.

The ability itself is non-transferable.

The information obtained may be sold or traded.

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Keep the champagne action secret

Strong success:
- Polly is not publicly/finally identified as the person who dosed Julian’s champagne.

Partial success:
- people suspect her, but cannot confidently prove the act.

Failure:
- the room correctly reconstructs that Polly deliberately used Aster-9.

This objective is about her act, not whether it killed Julian.

### 2. Work out whether she actually caused Julian’s death

This objective should become achievable around the first major forensic reversal.

Polly succeeds if she correctly understands:
- Aster-9 was not the fatal mechanism;
- Julian died later from something else.

She does not need the complete Cole/Rita solution for this objective, although she still participates in the universal final accusation.

### 3. Protect the political-access file

Success means the damaging file does not become broadly public.

Selling/showing it privately is not automatically failure.

A formal public release / registration / room-wide reveal is failure.

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Act I — VERY HIGH

Polly should look genuinely dangerous.

The strongest early theory may be:

> Polly poisoned Julian with Aster-9 and killed him.

This is intentionally plausible.

Do not undermine it prematurely.

### ~18:20 — FIRST REVERSAL

Release `FOR-002`.

The message must be clear:

> Aster-9 was present, but it did not cause Julian’s death.

This should be a real shock.

Polly now learns she attempted murder but probably did not cause the homicide.

### Act II onward

Polly’s direct homicide suspicion should fall.

Her criminal/moral exposure remains serious because:
- she intended to kill Julian;
- she actually dosed him.

Do not let players equate “not fatal” with “nothing happened.”

---

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDANCE FOR GM

Polly may be the player most tempted to ask the GM:

> “Did I kill him?”

Do not answer before the game’s evidence establishes it.

Before the forensic reversal, the correct GM stance is:

- confirm only what Polly canonically knows;
- do not reassure;
- do not imply she succeeded;
- do not imply she failed.

Once `FOR-002` is released, the game itself answers the causal question.

If Polly confesses early, allow it.

The mystery still works because:
- she is not the actual fatal killer;
- the first reversal forces the investigation onward.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Polly is a spine role because the Act I poison theory depends on her action.

If the player cannot attend:

Do not remove Aster-9 from the plot.

Before the party, either:

1. reassign Polly to another attendee; or
2. convert her action into an unplayed political-affairs NPC / documented actor and preserve:
   - poison evidence;
   - witness observation;
   - nonfatal reversal.

Reassignment is preferable because the false-killer experience is fun and socially valuable.

Yes.

No.

Not as part of the murder plot at the start.

Not as a criminal plot at the start.

### “Did Polly enter Julian’s office?”
No.

Yes.

## FINAL REVEAL FUNCTION

Polly should appear early in the chronological reveal.

Recommended sequence:

1. Polly deliberately put Aster-9 into Julian’s champagne intending to kill him.
2. Julian drank and became unwell.
3. Aster-9 was real evidence — but it was not fatal.
4. The later blackout / Sally / Neraval sequence explains why the initial poison theory was incomplete.

Recommended framing:

> Polly tried to kill Julian. She just wasn’t the person who actually did.

Do not turn that into comic absolution.

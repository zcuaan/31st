## CORE TRUTH

Penny is not involved in Julian’s murder.

Penny’s key structural roles are:

- two indivisible succession votes;
- old governance / ownership context;
- witness to Cole’s genuine physical alibi for the Neraval swap window.

At approximately **16:59**, Penny is in the Board Lounge with:

- Cole Control;
- Hugh Stake.

A photograph is taken.

This proves Cole did not personally perform the physical swap at that moment.

It does **not** prove Cole was uninvolved in the murder plan.

## POWER — LEGACY ACCESS

Once per game, Penny chooses one currently available governance, ownership or historic corporate item.

The GM gives the prepared **legacy / historical context note**.

This may clarify:
- how a governance mechanism used to work;
- historical ownership context;
- precedent;
- old emergency authority;
- why an apparently strange legacy clause exists.

It may NOT:
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- invent a new legal right;
- identify the murderer directly;
- override the succession rules.

### 1. Shape the succession
Penny succeeds if she meaningfully influences the outcome through:

# GM FILE — AL GORITHM
## Quant / Systems Architect

**Player-facing file:** `characters/12-al-gorithm.md`  
**Core function:** harmless system anomaly / timeline interpreter  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Al made an unauthorised system/model change.

It is real.

It is not the blackout.

It is not part of Julian’s murder.

The role exists to stop the game treating every technical anomaly as the same event.

Al helps players distinguish:
- Al’s unrelated anomaly;
- Opal’s deliberate blackout activity;
- later system activity proving Julian remained alive.

---

## WHAT AL GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Al knows:
- they made an unauthorised model/system change;
- the change may appear in technical review;
- it is unrelated to murder;
- Phil understands the security layer;
- the blackout looks different from Al’s experiment;
- later system timestamps may matter to the death timeline.

Al does not begin knowing the hidden murder solution.

---

## WHAT AL DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Al does not know:
- Opal caused the blackout;
- Phil created the backdoor;
- Vince commissioned Opal;
- Cole exploited the weak-security window;
- Rita swapped the Neraval;
- Polly used Aster-9;
- Sally entered the office;
- the complete later activity sequence.

---

## EVIDENCE CONNECTIONS

**`SYS-004`** — Al’s system/model anomaly  
- should initially look potentially relevant;
- later resolves as unrelated to the fatal mechanism.

**`SYS-001`** — wearable activity  
- later helps prove Julian was alive after Sally left.

**`SYS-002`** — office-system event  
- another independent late-activity route.

Al may help interpret these but must not be the only route to them.

---

## POWER — SYSTEMS ANALYSIS

Once per game, Al chooses one currently available item tagged:

`SYSTEM`

The GM gives one prepared systems-analysis result for free.

It may clarify:
- what kind of event it is;
- whether it is consistent with routine behaviour;
- whether two system events are technically distinct;
- timeline significance.

It may NOT:
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- create a log;
- reveal murderer identity directly.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Clear Al’s own change
Success means the room understands:
- Al made an unauthorised change;
- it was not the blackout or murder mechanism.

Do not require total reputational innocence.

### 2. Identify the system event that matters
Strong success means Al correctly identifies at least one later system/wearable event that materially changes the death timeline.

The key insight:
Julian was still active after Sally left.

---

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Early
Al’s anomaly creates technical noise.

### Middle
Players learn the blackout is deliberate and separate.

### Later
System evidence becomes useful for timeline reconstruction.

Al transitions from red herring to interpreter.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Al is absence-safe.

If absent:
- `SYS-004` can be explained by prepared analysis;
- `SYS-001` / `SYS-002` still carry the timeline independently.

---

## FINAL QA

Al passes if:
- their anomaly is real but nonfatal;
- system evidence is understandable without technical expertise;
- they help distinguish events rather than add a new system puzzle;
- power resolves quickly.

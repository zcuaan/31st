# GM FILE — LEE VERAGE
## Private Equity Partner / Board Director

**Player-facing file:** `characters/08-lee-verage.md`  
**Core function:** succession rival / lawful-removal red herring  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Lee had a secret plan to remove Julian Vane from power.

The plan was lawful.

It involved governance, board action and succession preparation.

Lee did not plan Julian’s death.

Lee did not know about Cole/Rita’s murder plot.

The intended distinction is:

> **Planning to remove someone from office is not the same as planning to kill them.**

---

## LAWFUL REMOVAL PLAN

Lee worked privately with **Clara Clause** before the gala.

The plan explored how the board could lawfully:

- constrain Julian’s authority;
- suspend or remove him;
- reorganise leadership;
- prepare a succession process.

This material should look suspicious if discovered before context is known.

It becomes less sinister once Clara/legal evidence establishes its lawful purpose.

Do not turn it into a fake murder clue.

---

## WHAT LEE GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Lee knows:

- Julian’s governance had become unstable;
- Helix was creating serious pressure;
- Max Ledger is a major CEO rival;
- Hugh and Penny are major succession power brokers;
- Clara knows the removal plan;
- Lee had been preparing for a Vantage without Julian in charge.

---

## WHAT LEE DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Lee does not know:

- Cole planned the murder;
- Rita helped Cole;
- Polly used Aster-9;
- Sally entered the office;
- Opal caused the blackout;
- Vince commissioned Opal;
- the Neraval mechanism.

---

## EVIDENCE CONNECTIONS

Relevant side evidence:
- Clara’s lawful-removal material;
- succession/governance documents;
- any board preparation notes.

Final IDs remain **TBD** until the master evidence registry is locked.

Do not create a unique murder-essential clue around Lee.

---

## POWER — BOARDROOM READ

Once per game, after succession opens, Lee chooses one voting shareholder.

The GM gives Lee a short prepared note on that voter’s:

- priorities;
- pressure points;
- likely concerns;
- what kind of promise or concession they may value.

The note does **not** reveal the actual vote.

It is advisory, not predictive.

It cannot be used to bypass secret ballot mechanics.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Become Interim CEO
Normal nomination and voting rules apply.

### 2. Keep the removal plan private until succession opens
Success means the room does not broadly establish the existence/nature of Lee’s removal plan before succession begins.

After succession opens, Lee may reveal it strategically.

---

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Early
Lee is a plausible suspect because:
- he wanted Julian gone;
- he had a plan;
- he benefits from succession.

### Middle
The legal/governance context should clarify:
- the plan was real;
- it was not murder.

### Late
Lee remains politically dangerous but homicide suspicion should fall away.

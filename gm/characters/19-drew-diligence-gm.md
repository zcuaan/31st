## POWER — DOCUMENT COMPARISON

Once per game, Drew chooses two currently available related document versions.

The GM gives the prepared **document comparison**.

The result may identify:
- changed wording;
- removed caveat;
- altered assumption;
- changed date;
- changed figure;
- changed approval/sign-off.

It may NOT:
- invent a version;
- unlock unreleased documents;
- decide motive automatically;
- identify the murderer.

This power should reward careful selection of the right pair.

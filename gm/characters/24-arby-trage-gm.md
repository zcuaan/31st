## POWER — OFF-MARKET OFFER

Once per game, Arby gives the GM a written offer containing:

1. **Target**
2. **Amount offered**
3. **Desired return**

The GM privately delivers the offer anonymously.

The target chooses:

- **ACCEPT**
- **REJECT**

If accepted:
- complete the transfer / Deal Slip as appropriate.

If rejected:
- the power is spent.

There is **no GM-mediated counter-offer loop**.

The recipient may later approach Arby normally if they infer or learn the sender.

### Limits

The offer may request:

- information;
- a portable eligible item;
- succession support;
- a nomination signature;
- a signed promise;
- another game-legal concession.

It may NOT request:

- real property;
- unreleased GM information;
- another character’s non-transferable power;
- guaranteed secret-ballot compliance;
- impossible actions.

If the requested return is invalid, tell Arby before consuming the power.

# GM FILE — RITA DISTRIBUTION
## Chief of Staff, Vantage House Group

**Player-facing file:** `characters/02-rita-distribution.md`  
**Core function:** knowing accomplice / physical swapper / institutional-political foil  
**Murder involvement:** ACTUAL ACCOMPLICE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Rita Distribution knowingly helped Cole Control murder Julian Vane.

Cole is the architect.

Rita is the knowing accomplice and physical executor of the device swap.

She did **not** invent the murder plan.

She did **not** source the counterfeit Neraval.

She did **not** create the blackout.

She did **not** poison Julian’s champagne.

She did **not** steal Julian’s USB.

Her specific fatal contribution is:

> **Rita knowingly replaced Julian’s genuine Neraval with the counterfeit device during the degraded-security window, then hid the genuine device in the Event Operations Case.**

This is not an accidental assistance role.

Rita knew the replacement was intended to kill Julian.

---

## POLITICAL / THEMATIC FUNCTION

Rita is openly communist.

She works extremely close to elite corporate power and is well paid.

That tension is intentional.

Her politics are not a clue to murder.

Her ideology does not make her guilty.

Her actual actions do.

The thematic distinction is:

> **Being right about structural problems does not automatically make what you do about them right.**

Rita genuinely believes:

- founder power at Vantage is dangerously concentrated;
- Julian created or tolerated many of the conditions he now wants to expose;
- replacing one CEO may not meaningfully change the hierarchy;
- Julian was preparing to recast himself as the brave reformer while others absorbed the blame.

Those beliefs may be sincerely held.

They do not excuse her knowing participation in homicide.

---

## COLE / RITA RELATIONSHIP

Cole believes Julian’s concentration of power has become institutionally intolerable.

Cole initially frames the problem as governance.

His logic radicalises:

remove the power → remove the person.

Rita’s critique is broader.

Useful GM shorthand:

> **Cole wants to depose the king. Rita wants to abolish the throne. They agree on one thing: Julian has to go.**

Rita knowingly helps Cole.

She is not manipulated into the murder.

She is not surprised by the fatal intent.

## EXACT CANONICAL TIMELINE — RITA

### Before the gala
Rita knows:

- Cole intends Julian to die;
- Cole has arranged a counterfeit Neraval;
- she is expected to perform the physical replacement;
- Cole expects a short degraded-security window.

Rita does **not** know exactly how Cole learned that this window would occur.

### 16:55
Opal Ration initiates the security disruption using Phil McCracken’s unauthorised backdoor.

Rita does not know the Opal/Vince insider-information crime.

### 16:57
Rita leaves the gala under a plausible event/security/logistics pretext.

This is consistent with her Chief of Staff role.

### 16:58
Using legitimate Chief of Staff access, Rita reaches the relevant private area.

She:

1. removes Julian’s genuine Neraval;
2. replaces it with the counterfeit Neraval.

She knows the counterfeit is intended to kill Julian.

### 16:59
Cole is photographed in the Board Lounge with Hugh Stake and Penny Sterling.

This genuinely shows Cole was not physically performing the swap.

### 17:00
Rita hides the genuine Neraval in the **Event Operations Case**.

The case remains sealed / not in play until Act III.

### 17:02
Rita is back in public.

### 17:12
Julian later uses the counterfeit Neraval himself.

Rita is not present for the activation.

### ~17:28
Julian actually dies.

### 17:32
Perry Spin goes looking for Julian and gets Rita because access to the private area is restricted.

### 17:34
Perry and Rita enter and discover Julian dead.

Rita already knows what she expects has happened.

### 17:35
Death is announced.

Rita now has to behave like the competent Chief of Staff managing the crisis.

## RITA’S PUBLIC STORY

Rita’s safest cover story is mostly true.

She can truthfully say:

- she was working throughout the gala;
- she moved between event/executive areas;
- she had legitimate access;
- there was a security disruption;
- she was handling logistics;
- she later accompanied Perry to Julian’s office;
- she discovered Julian’s body with Perry.

What she must omit:

- the 16:58 Neraval swap;
- hiding the genuine device;
- her knowledge of Cole’s plan;
- their coordination.

Do not give Rita a perfect false alibi.

Her strength is that her real role naturally explains a lot of suspicious movement.

## POWER — CHIEF OF STAFF ACCESS

Once per game, Rita chooses one **10-minute window before Julian’s death**.

The GM gives her the prepared **official diary / logistics record** for that period.

Important:

This is an **official record**, not omniscient truth.

It may show:
- scheduled meetings;
- logged movements;
- official room usage;
- event logistics;
- known staff assignments.

It may NOT:
- reveal hidden actions simply because they happened;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- identify the murderer directly;
- invent a new record.

If the chosen period has no prepared result, tell Rita before consuming the power.

The ability itself is non-transferable.

The information obtained may be sold or traded.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Keep her role in Julian’s death secret
Full success means Rita avoids being correctly identified as knowing accomplice / swapper in the final reconstruction.

Partial success is possible if players suspect her but cannot reconstruct her actual action.

### 2. Protect Cole’s involvement
Rita succeeds best if Cole is not correctly identified as architect/mastermind.

She should not be forced to sacrifice herself for Cole.

Self-preservation comes first if necessary.

### 3. Get the next CEO to make two concrete structural/governance commitments
These should be specific.

Examples:
- independent audit authority;
- employee representation;
- stronger board oversight;
- limits on executive unilateral power;
- transparent control exceptions;
- protected whistleblowing / reporting channels.

The two commitments may come from one candidate or multiple candidates, but at least one should come from the eventual winner for a strong success.

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Act I — LOW TO MODERATE
Rita looks useful, competent and busy.

Her closeness to Julian makes her interesting, but nothing should immediately identify her.

### Act II — MODERATE
The blackout / access environment makes legitimate-access people more relevant.

Rita’s movements can begin to matter.

Do not dump her credential evidence too early.

### Act III — HIGH
Rita should come under real pressure when these start converging:

- genuine Neraval found in Event Operations;
- access around blackout;
- counterfeit device confirmation;
- her legitimate ability to reach the area;
- coordination fragments with Cole.

At this stage, Rita’s strongest defence is:

> legitimate access is not proof of what she did with it.

That defence is true but incomplete.

### Endgame
Players should be able to conclude:

- Rita physically performed the swap;
- she knew the fatal intent;
- Cole planned/arranged the operation.

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDANCE FOR GM

Rita is a difficult role.

The player must be allowed to lie.

Do not force nervousness.

Do not treat hesitation as evidence.

Do not “help” investigators by interpreting Rita’s behaviour.

If the player gives an excellent technically-true answer, let it stand.

Example:

> “Did you have access to that area?”

Rita may answer:
> “Of course. I was Julian’s Chief of Staff.”

That is truthful.

The game should convict her through evidence, not GM tone.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Rita is a **critical spine role**.

If the player cannot attend:

Do not delete Rita’s action.

Before the party, choose one contingency:

1. reassign Rita to a reliable attendee; or
2. convert the physical swap into an unplayed Chief-of-Staff NPC action and move the necessary social clues into documentary evidence.

Do not improvise the replacement during the party.

The following must survive:
- actual swap;
- genuine device hidden in Event Operations;
- Cole/Rita coordination route;
- legitimate-access clue.

Yes.

No.

No. Cole arranged it.

### “Did Rita know it was meant to kill Julian?”
Yes.

No. Julian later used it himself.

### “Did Rita know about Polly / Sally / Opal?”
Not at the start.

### “Was Rita with Perry when the body was discovered?”
Yes. They entered together at approximately 17:34.

## FINAL REVEAL FUNCTION

Reveal Rita after the fatal mechanism is understood.

Recommended sequence:

1. the scene Neraval was counterfeit;
2. the genuine Neraval was hidden in Event Operations;
3. Rita had the legitimate access and opportunity to perform the swap;
4. Rita knowingly replaced it during the blackout;
5. Cole planned/arranged the murder.

Do not frame Rita as merely following orders.

She knowingly chose to participate.

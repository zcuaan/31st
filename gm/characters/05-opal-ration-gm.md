# GM FILE — OPAL RATION
## Operations & Special Projects

**Player-facing file:** `characters/05-opal-ration.md`  
**Core function:** blackout culprit for separate crime / accidental murder enabler / CEO wild-card  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Opal Ration deliberately caused the security disruption.

Her motive was **corporate information theft**, not murder.

Vince Ture commissioned her to obtain confidential Helix information.

Opal used an unauthorised backdoor already present in Vantage’s systems.

She did not create that backdoor.

She did not know Cole Control had noticed the weak-security window.

She did not know Rita Distribution would use the window to swap Julian’s Neraval.

Critical distinction:

> **Phil created the vulnerability. Opal exploited it. Cole exploited Opal exploiting it.**

That is the clean causal chain.

## POWER — OPERATIONS TRACE

Once per game, Opal chooses one currently available **operational or digital item**.

The GM provides the prepared **handling / process history**.

This may clarify:

- which operational workflow touched the item;
- where it moved;
- which team or function owned a step;
- whether a route was routine, unusual or manually bypassed.

It may NOT:

- reveal unreleased evidence;
- identify the murderer directly;
- invent system records;
- expose private facts beyond the prepared result.

If no prepared result exists, tell Opal before consuming the power.

The ability is non-transferable.

The result may be sold or traded.

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Act I — LOW
The blackout initially looks like infrastructure noise.

### Act II — HIGH
Digital evidence establishes:

- deliberate execution;
- backdoor;
- Operations trace;
- Opal/Vince connection.

Players may reasonably think:

> Opal caused the blackout so she could kill Julian unseen.

That is wrong but plausible.

### Act III
The murder case should move from:

> who caused the blackout?

to:

> who exploited the blackout for the fatal act?

Opal remains guilty of the side crime.

She is not cleared morally or legally.

She is simply not the murderer.

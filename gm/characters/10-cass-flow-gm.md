# GM FILE — CASS FLOW
## Hedge Fund Investor

**Player-facing file:** `characters/10-cass-flow.md`  
**Core function:** information market-maker / monetisation engine / succession swing voter  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Cass has no murder secret.

V2 removes the older anonymous-fraud-offer subplot.

Cass is deliberately streamlined into:

- money;
- information trading;
- one succession vote;
- market interpretation.

This keeps Cass useful without creating another side mystery.

---

## WHAT CASS GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Cass knows:

- Vantage/Helix information could materially affect value;
- Max is heavily tied to the growth story;
- Sally may possess useful negative research;
- Vince may trade aggressively;
- Hugh controls a major voting bloc;
- Cass’s single vote may matter in close succession arithmetic.

---

## POWER — MARKET CHECK

Once per game, Cass chooses one currently available item tagged:

`FINANCIAL`

The GM gives Cass the prepared **market / commercial significance note**.

The result may clarify:

- whether the information is likely material;
- who might care commercially;
- whether it affects valuation / transaction risk / bargaining leverage;
- whether the market implication is large, moderate or limited.

It may NOT:
- predict a real stock price;
- create a new fact;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- identify the murderer directly.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Finish at least £300k richer
Use ordinary Vantage Money only.

Profit = ending personal cash − starting personal cash.

No separate investment-return mechanic.

### 2. Obtain a meaningful concession for the vote
This may be:
- cash;
- information;
- signed governance promise;
- future-business commitment;
- other concrete value.

A vague compliment does not count.

---

## SUCCESSION FUNCTION

Cass holds **1 vote**.

Cass may:
- sign nomination papers;
- accept bribes;
- promise support;
- betray promises;
- vote secretly.

Do not reveal Cass’s actual ballot through any power.

## INTENDED PLAY ARC

### Early
Cass buys and sells information.

### Middle
Financial scandal increases information value.

### Succession
Cass turns one vote into leverage.

### Late
Cass may monetise murder information, candidate risk or Helix knowledge.

No extra side plot is required.

Cass passes if:

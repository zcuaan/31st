## POWER — RISK MODEL

Once per game, Vera chooses one currently available financial/modelling/risk item.

The GM gives the prepared **assumption / sensitivity analysis**.

This may show:
- key assumptions;
- how sensitive a conclusion is to them;
- whether a base case is unusually optimistic;
- what changes under a more conservative scenario.

It may NOT:
- invent new figures;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- make real-world financial predictions;
- identify the murderer directly.

### 1. Prove her warnings were justified

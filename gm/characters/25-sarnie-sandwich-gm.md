# GM FILE — SARNIE SANDWICH
## Event Hospitality & Catering

**Core function:** truthful observational witness  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE RULE

Sarnie never lies.

Sarnie is also never omniscient.

The exact witness observations are fixed.

Do not improvise additional useful sightings.

---

## LOCKED OBSERVATIONS

### `WIT-001`
Sarnie saw **Polly Tician behaving unusually around Julian’s champagne**.

This supports suspicion.

It does **not** establish exactly what Polly did.

---

### `WIT-002`
Sarnie saw Julian become **distracted / unsteady after drinking**.

This supports the early Aster-9 theory.

It does not prove causation.

---

### `WIT-003`
Sarnie saw **Sally Short leave Julian’s office/private area looking panicked**.

This supports the Act II Sally theory.

It does not prove Sally killed Julian.

---

### `WIT-004`
Sarnie heard **unmistakable movement/noise from Julian’s office after Sally had already left**.

This supports the second reversal:

> Julian was alive after Sally left.

This conclusion must also be available through system / forensic evidence.

Sarnie is not the sole route.

---

## DELIVERY RULE

Sarnie does not volunteer all four observations at once.

Players should need to ask.

Good question:
> “Did you see anyone near Julian’s champagne?”

Answer:
> Polly was behaving unusually around it.

Vague question:
> “Did you see anything weird?”

Natural answer:
> “A few things, honestly. What are you asking about?”

This encourages actual interviewing without turning Sarnie into a riddle gate.

---

## TIMELINE ANCHORS

Relevant fictional sequence:

- ~16:40 Polly acts near champagne
- ~16:45 Julian drinks
- ~16:50 Julian visibly unsteady
- 17:17 Sally enters office
- ~17:20 Sally leaves
- 17:23 Sarnie hears movement/noise
- 17:25 system later records activity
- ~17:28 Julian dies

Sarnie does not need to quote exact minute numbers unless specifically asked and the GM wants to support timing.

A natural answer may be:

> “It was definitely after Sally had left.”

---

## PERFORMANCE GUIDANCE

Sarnie should feel like:

- an ordinary event worker;
- someone who notices more than guests realise;
- calm;
- not theatrical;
- not secretly brilliant.

Avoid “mystery witness” acting.

The fun comes from players realising they should interview the catering staff.

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

If live Sarnie is unavailable:

- convert each locked observation into a written witness statement;
- release only when appropriate;
- preserve redundancy for `WIT-004`.

Do not remove the observations entirely.

---

Sarnie passes if:

- every answer is truthful;
- observations stay limited;
- players benefit from specific questioning;
- no essential conclusion depends only on Sarnie;
- Sarnie never accidentally spoils the Neraval solution.

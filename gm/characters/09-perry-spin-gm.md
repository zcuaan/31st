## POWER — SPIN

Once per game, Perry chooses one piece of **registered/public evidence**.

He writes a statement of 30 words or fewer.

GM publishes it publicly as clearly labelled:

- commentary;
- campaign messaging;
- crisis communications;
- or equivalent.

It must never be presented as official evidence.

Perry may:
- minimise;
- exaggerate significance;
- imply alternative interpretation;
- redirect attention.

Perry may not:
- alter evidence text;
- forge a source;
- invent new facts;
- claim the GM verified his interpretation.

This power should resolve almost instantly.

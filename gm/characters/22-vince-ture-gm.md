# GM FILE — VINCE TURE
## Crypto Founder / Venture Capitalist

**Player-facing file:** `characters/22-vince-ture.md`  
**Core function:** Opal’s buyer / Helix-information monetiser / outsider CEO candidate  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Vince Ture commissioned Opal Ration to obtain confidential Helix information.

Vince is guilty of knowingly commissioning improper information theft.

He did not:

- plan Julian’s murder;
- know Cole/Rita’s plan;
- know Opal’s exact technical method in advance;
- know the blackout would become part of the fatal timeline.

Vince’s role is a side-crime connector, not a murder conspirator.

---

## WHAT VINCE GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Vince knows:

- he paid Opal for confidential Helix information;
- Opal successfully obtained targeted material;
- the information is potentially valuable;
- the acquisition method was improper;
- Vince does not know the full technical route;
- Cass and Arby may value the information;
- Vince may run for CEO.

---

## WHAT VINCE DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Vince does not know:

- Phil created the backdoor;
- Opal caused the blackout by exploiting it;
- Cole knew the weak-security window would occur;
- Rita used the window for the Neraval swap;
- Polly used Aster-9;
- Sally entered the office;
- the Neraval mechanism.

## POWER — DEAL FLOW

Once per game, Vince chooses one currently available item tagged:

- `CORPORATE`, or
- `FINANCIAL`.

The GM gives the prepared **commercial-value / deal-context note**.

This may clarify:
- who might value the information;
- whether it creates transaction leverage;
- whether it could alter bargaining power;
- whether its value depends on secrecy/timing.

It may NOT:
- invent a buyer;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- create new deal terms;
- identify the murderer directly.

### 1. Keep Opal commission hidden until CEO vote

Keep it simple.

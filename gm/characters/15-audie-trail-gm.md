## POWER — SOURCE CHECK

Once per game, Audie chooses one currently available numeric, financial or corporate document.

The GM provides the prepared **source reconciliation**.

This may state:
- matches source;
- partially matches;
- materially diverges;
- uses altered assumptions;
- omits relevant source context.

It may NOT:
- invent figures;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- reveal murderer identity;
- create a new source record.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Establish the mismatch
Success means Audie connects internal source evidence to at least one independent corroborating route.

### 2. Win independent audit / whistleblower commitment
A strong success requires a concrete promise from a credible CEO candidate, ideally the eventual winner.

Examples:
- protected whistleblower escalation;
- independent internal audit access;
- board reporting line;
- audit sign-off before major transaction reporting.

No extra mechanic is needed.

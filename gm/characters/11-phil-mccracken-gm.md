# GM FILE — PHIL MCCRACKEN
## Chief Information Security Officer

**Player-facing file:** `characters/11-phil-mccracken.md`  
**Core function:** vulnerability creator / technical red herring / route to Opal  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Phil created the unauthorised backdoor that Opal later exploited.

Phil did not:

- cause the blackout;
- know Opal planned to exploit the backdoor;
- know Vince Ture commissioned the Helix theft;
- know Cole Control had noticed the weak-security window;
- know Rita Distribution would use that window;
- participate in Julian’s murder.

The clean causal distinction is:

> **Phil created the vulnerability. Opal exploited it. Cole exploited Opal exploiting it.**

Phil is culpable for bad security governance, not homicide.

---

## BACKDOOR TRUTH

The backdoor / shortcut was created before the gala.

Phil’s motive was convenience / operational speed.

It was unauthorised.

It should feel:
- plausible;
- embarrassing;
- professionally serious;
- not malicious by itself.

Do not add technical implementation detail that turns this into actionable cyber instruction.

The game only needs:
- it existed;
- Phil created it;
- Opal later used it;
- the blackout was deliberate.

---

## WHAT PHIL GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Phil knows:

- they created the unauthorised backdoor;
- it existed before the gala;
- they did not execute the blackout;
- the blackout looks deliberate rather than random;
- somebody else appears to have exploited the shortcut;
- Al understands the surrounding system environment;
- Audit & Risk will treat the backdoor as a governance failure.

---

## WHAT PHIL DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Phil does not know:

- Opal caused the blackout;
- Vince commissioned Opal;
- Cole knew the window would happen;
- Rita used the window for the swap;
- Cole planned the murder;
- Polly used Aster-9;
- Sally entered the office;
- the Neraval mechanism.

---

## EVIDENCE CONNECTIONS

**`DIG-002`** — unauthorised backdoor/configuration  
- establishes the vulnerability;
- ultimately links to Phil as creator.

**`DIG-003`** — deliberate execution of the disruption  
- separates Phil’s creation of the vulnerability from the later act of exploitation.

**`DIG-004`** — Operations credential trace  
- moves suspicion from vulnerability creator toward Opal as exploiter.

Phil should never be the unique route to identifying Opal.

Documentary evidence must carry the distinction if Phil is absent or uncooperative.

---

## POWER — DIGITAL RECOVERY

Once per game, Phil chooses one currently available digital/metadata question or eligible digital item.

The GM gives Phil one prepared **digital recovery / metadata result** for free.

This can include:
- metadata;
- timestamp context;
- system provenance;
- simple digital recovery result;
- whether a digital event is consistent with ordinary use or deliberate action.

It may NOT:
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- create a new log;
- reveal the murderer directly;
- invent technical capability.

If no prepared result exists, tell Phil before consuming the power.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Identify the exploiter
Phil succeeds if they correctly establish that Opal exploited the backdoor / caused the deliberate disruption.

They do not need to solve the murder for this objective.

### 2. Secure amnesty
This can be a signed promise from:
- the incoming CEO;
- Cole / governance leadership before the vote;
- another credible authority figure.

The promise should be concrete enough to mean:
- no dismissal;
- formal remediation rather than punishment;
- protected disclosure / remediation path;
or similar.

No special amnesty mechanic is needed.

---

## INTENDED SUSPICION ARC

### Early
Phil may look suspicious once the backdoor appears.

### Middle
`DIG-003` and `DIG-004` should establish:
- the disruption was deliberate;
- Phil created the route;
- someone else actually used it.

### Late
Phil becomes useful technical context rather than a murder suspect.

Do not let “not the exploiter” turn into “the backdoor was fine.”

It was not fine.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Phil is not a murder-spine role, but the vulnerability chain must survive.

If absent:
- `DIG-002` identifies the backdoor provenance;
- `DIG-003` shows deliberate execution;
- `DIG-004` points toward Opal.

No critical conclusion may depend on Phil confessing.

## FINAL QA

Phil passes if:
- creator and exploiter are clearly distinguished;
- technical detail stays non-actionable;
- their bad governance decision remains real;
- their power resolves quickly;
- Opal remains independently discoverable.

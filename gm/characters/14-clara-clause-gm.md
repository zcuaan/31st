# GM FILE — CLARA CLAUSE
## M&A Lawyer

**Player-facing file:** `characters/14-clara-clause.md`  
**Core function:** legal-context interpreter / Lee corroborator / Helix pressure  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Clara helped Lee Verage prepare a lawful route to remove or constrain Julian Vane.

This work is intentionally suspicious-looking.

It is not a murder plan.

Clara also knows Julian asked about the consequences of suspending Helix.

Her role is to help players distinguish:

- legal/governance preparation;
- transaction consequences;
- actual evidence of homicide.

---

## WHAT CLARA GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Clara knows:

- Julian was considering serious action on Helix;
- Julian asked about consequences of suspending/stopping the transaction;
- Lee had asked for a lawful removal/governance route;
- Lee’s plan was not violent;
- documents may look incriminating without legal context.

---

## WHAT CLARA DOES NOT KNOW AT START

Clara does not know:
- Cole planned the murder;
- Rita helped Cole;
- Polly used Aster-9;
- Sally entered the office;
- Opal caused the blackout;
- Vince commissioned Opal;
- the fatal Neraval mechanism.

---

## EVIDENCE CONNECTIONS

**`LEG-001`** — Clara’s note / Julian asks about consequences of suspending Helix  
- demonstrates Julian’s seriousness before death.

Related lawful-removal material:
- Lee/Clara governance plan;
- final evidence ID remains **TBD** until master registry is locked.

Clara should be able to explain why the removal plan is lawful without instantly removing all suspicion from Lee.

---

## POWER — LEGAL REVIEW

Once per game, Clara chooses one currently available legal/governance/transaction item.

The GM provides the prepared **legal / governance analysis**.

This may clarify:
- what a clause or document authorises;
- whether a step is routine, unusual or legally significant;
- whether a document implies intent or merely preserves an option;
- governance consequences.

It may NOT:
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- invent legal rules;
- identify the murderer directly;
- create a new document.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Preserve the Helix legal record
Success means the relevant legal/transaction record remains available and accurately represented.

Clara should resist:
- destruction;
- misleading summaries;
- false claims about what documents say.

### 2. Keep Lee’s removal plan private until succession opens
Success means the plan is not broadly/publicly established before succession begins.

After that, Clara may reveal it strategically.

---

## INTENDED PLAY ARC

### Early
Clara is useful but not central.

### Middle
Lee’s plan and Helix documents make her valuable.

### Succession
She becomes a credible interpreter of governance promises and board mechanics.

### Late
She helps players avoid confusing legal preparation with murder evidence.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Clara is absence-safe.

If absent:
- `LEG-001` and the lawful-removal documents carry her function;
- Lee’s plan must remain independently interpretable.

---

## FINAL QA

Clara passes if:
- she adds logical/legal challenge rather than exposition;
- Lee’s plan stays suspicious but lawful;
- Helix pressure remains clear;
- Legal Review resolves quickly;
- no real-world legal advice is required.

# GM FILE — LUCY LEAKS
## Corporate Intelligence Adviser

**Player-facing file:** `characters/17-lucy-leaks.md`  
**Core function:** information broker / dual-client secrecy / Julian personal-history flavour  
**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Lucy is not involved in Julian’s murder.

Her main gameplay functions are:

- information trading;
- succession intelligence;
- dual-client secrecy;
- a personal-history link to Julian that creates social ambiguity without creating murder evidence.

Lucy was once in a serious relationship with Julian.

That history is real.

It is not a hidden murder motive.

---

## DUAL CLIENTS

Before the gala:

### Hugh Stake
Hugh privately hired Lucy to map succession factions and candidate vulnerabilities.

### Lee Verage
Lee separately hired Lucy to understand the voting landscape, rival candidates and useful influence points.

Neither client knows the other also hired Lucy.

Lucy knows she is balancing two clients.

This is a social/commercial secret, not a murder clue.

---

## WHAT LUCY GENUINELY KNOWS AT START

Lucy knows:

- Hugh hired her;
- Lee hired her;
- neither knows about the other;
- Hugh has major voting power;
- Lee is a serious succession contender;
- Julian’s death makes intelligence more valuable;
- Julian was once her serious partner.

Lucy does not begin with privileged knowledge of the murder.

---

## WHAT LUCY DOES NOT KNOW AT START

- Vince commissioned Opal;

---

## POWER — BACKGROUND CHECK

Once per game, Lucy chooses one player character.

The GM gives Lucy one prepared **non-murder-critical intelligence note** about that character.

The note may contain:

- public/professional history;
- known business exposure;
- reputation context;
- declared relationships;
- succession pressure;
- noncritical side-plot context.

It may NOT:

- identify the murderer;
- reveal a hidden murder action;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- invent a new secret;
- expose information that would collapse another character’s core mystery mechanic.

Prepare a short safe note for each likely target before party day.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Sell intelligence to at least two buyers for £300k+ total
Ordinary Vantage Money only.

The buyers must be different people.

The information may be:
- Background Check result;
- information Lucy learns socially;
- legitimate observations;
- other non-public information she acquires.

### 2. Hide dual clients until CEO vote
Strong success:
- neither client publicly establishes that Lucy was simultaneously working for the other before the ballot.

Private suspicion does not automatically count as failure.

---

## JULIAN RELATIONSHIP

Keep this as character depth, not a new evidence tree.

The relationship may affect:

- reveal flavour.

Do not introduce a hidden breakup crime, revenge plot or unique murder clue.

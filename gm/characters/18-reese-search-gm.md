**Murder involvement:** NONE

---

## CORE TRUTH

Reese has no dark secret.

That is deliberate.

Reese is a lighter role designed to contribute meaningfully through:

- public research;
- corroboration;
- clue sharing;
- careful observation.

Reese must never be the sole route to a critical deduction.

---

## POWER — RESEARCH REQUEST

Once per game, Reese chooses one relevant:

- person;
- company;
- transaction;
- event;
- claim;

that could reasonably be checked in public records.

The GM gives one prepared **public-record research result** for free.

It may include:
- dates;
- public filings;
- public ownership;
- public event/sponsorship context;
- archived public statement;
- public-source contradiction.

It may NOT:
- reveal private records;
- unlock unreleased evidence;
- create a fictional secret out of nowhere;
- identify the murderer directly.

---

## OBJECTIVES — GM INTERPRETATION

### 1. Connect public research to independent evidence
Success means Reese links their Northstar/public-source finding to at least one separate source.

### 2. Get a useful finding into the wider investigation
Success means at least one material Reese finding is:
- registered;
- publicly shared;
- passed to investigators;
- or clearly used in another player’s reasoning.

---

## ROLE-SAFETY DESIGN

Reese is intentionally low-pressure.

The role does not require:

- public speaking;
- aggressive bargaining;
- lying;
- dominating a room;
- holding a unique critical clue.

Reese can contribute by quietly handing one strong piece of research to somebody else.

---

## ABSENCE CONTINGENCY

Reese is fully absence-safe.

If absent:
- `FIN-004` can enter through the evidence system;
- no core deduction breaks.

---

## FINAL QA

Reese passes if:
- the role feels useful despite lower cash/status;
- public-source work rewards careful players;
- no clue depends solely on Reese speaking up;
- Research Request resolves quickly;
- the role remains accessible and low-pressure.

Succession Governance Rules

  • Nomination Deposit: £200,000 non-refundable nomination deposit. The deposit is paid into the Vantage treasury and is not returned if the candidate loses or withdraws. This is not personal income for another player.
  • Signatures: Candidates require signatures from two voting shareholders to appear on the ballot.
  • Restriction: Candidates cannot nominate themselves. A nomination signature does not bind the voter to a final vote.
  • Majority Required: 5 votes.
  • Blocs: Olivier’s 3 votes and Jane’s 2 votes are indivisible blocs.
  • Runoff Procedure: If no candidate receives 5 votes, the top two proceed to a runoff. If tied for second, all tied candidates proceed. The lowest-scoring candidate is eliminated sequentially until a majority is reached.
  • Conduct: Suspicion or accusation of misconduct does not remove candidacy or voting rights.

Game Mechanics Clarifications

  • Evidence Canon: Official evidence published on this site is canonically genuine. However, players are completely free to claim that it is AI-generated, planted, manipulated, politically motivated, institutional propaganda, or taken out of context.

  • Abilities: Character abilities cannot be transferred or sold. Information obtained through them may be traded.

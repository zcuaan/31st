# VANTAGE HOUSE — GM CONTROL PACK V1
## SECTION 2 — WHATSAPP ANNOUNCEMENTS

**Purpose:** Ready-to-paste official messages for the Vantage House WhatsApp group.

### House style
Every official message begins:

**VANTAGE OFFICIAL UPDATE**

Keep official posts:
- factual;
- short;
- slightly corporate / ominous;
- free of GM interpretation;
- spoiler-safe.

Players may argue, joke, lie and speculate underneath them. The official account does not debate.

### Status key
- **SEND** — recommended standard message.
- **CONDITIONAL** — send only when the stated trigger occurs.
- **OPTIONAL** — useful, but not required for the game to function.

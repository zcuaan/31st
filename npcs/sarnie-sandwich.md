# Sarnie Sandwich
## Private Caterer to London's Financial Elite
### Played by: Sahani

**Character vibe:** warm, observant  
**Role type:** Live NPC witness  
**Starting cash:** None  
**Can lie:** No — Sarnie's observations are always truthful

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Sarnie Sandwich.

You cater parties for people with:

- too much money;
- too many secrets;
- and very strong opinions about sparkling water.

Tonight you are catering the Vantage House Capital gala.

You are not part of the corporate drama.

You would very much prefer to finish serving the food.

Unfortunately, rich people behave extremely suspiciously when they think staff are not paying attention.

You were paying attention.

---

# HOW YOUR ROLE WORKS

You do NOT walk around announcing clues.

Carry on catering normally.

If somebody comes to speak to you, answer naturally.

Only reveal information relevant to what they actually ask.

You do not need exact wording.

If someone asks a sensible question about the topic, give them the clue.

---

# WHAT YOU SAW

## THE DRINK

If somebody asks about:

- Alexander's champagne;
- who was near his drink;
- whether anybody touched his glass;
- or anything suspicious around the drinks

tell them:

You remember seeing **Polly Tix** unusually close to Alexander's champagne while Alexander was distracted.

You did not actually see Polly put anything into it.

But you noticed because Polly was not collecting a drink of their own.

---

## ALEXANDER FEELING UNWELL

If somebody asks:

- when Alexander started seeming strange;
- whether he looked ill;
- whether anything happened before he went to his office

tell them:

Alexander was already behaving strangely **before he left the main party**.

He seemed confused and unsteady.

This began after he had been drinking from his champagne.

---

## CLAUDIA NEAR THE OFFICE

If somebody asks:

- who went near Alexander's office;
- whether you saw Claudia;
- about the service corridor;
- about anyone behaving suspiciously

tell them:

You saw **Claudia Short** coming away from the office/service-corridor area.

Claudia looked panicked.

This was before Alexander's body was officially discovered.

---

## THE MOST IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

If somebody asks:

- whether Alexander could still have been alive;
- what happened after Claudia left;
- whether you heard anything from the office;
- exactly what you noticed around that time

tell them:

Several minutes after Claudia left the area, you heard a clear noise from inside Alexander's office.

It sounded like:

- movement;
- followed by something hitting or falling against furniture.

You assumed Alexander was still inside.

You did not investigate.

You were working.

---

# IMPORTANT RULE

You are useful.

You are NOT essential.

Every important fact you know also exists elsewhere in the evidence network.

So do not chase players down and force clues on them.

If nobody thinks to interrogate the caterer, that is their problem.

---

# HOW TO PLAY SARNIE

Be normal.

You do not need to "act suspicious".

You can look mildly entertained that the guests have collectively lost their minds.

If people ask completely irrelevant questions, you can honestly say:

> "I don't know."

You are not Google.

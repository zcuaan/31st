# Amy N. Alysis
## Senior Forensic Due-Diligence Specialist, Helix
### Played by: Amy

**Character vibe:** serious, incisive  
**Role type:** Late-arriving NPC / Act III evidence delivery  
**Expected arrival:** Around 8pm  
**Starting cash:** None  
**Can lie:** No — Amy's professional findings are truthful

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Amy N. Alysis.

You work for Helix.

You have spent the evening reviewing Vantage House Capital's financial records because Helix was preparing to complete a major transaction with the company.

You were supposed to be reviewing numbers.

Instead, you appear to have discovered:

**a financial crime scene attached to an actual crime scene.**

Excellent.

---

# YOUR ARRIVAL

You arrive naturally whenever Amy actually gets here from work.

There is NO exact required arrival time.

Ideal window:

**approximately 7:45–8:20pm**

When you arrive, the Host may introduce you as:

> "Helix's forensic due-diligence specialist has arrived."

You then become available for questioning.

---

# WHAT YOU HAVE DISCOVERED

You have three major findings.

## FINDING 1 — THE NUMBERS ARE WRONG

Your review confirms that several Vantage figures used to support the Helix transaction cannot be reconciled with the underlying records.

This is not simply:

- optimistic forecasting;
- unusual accounting;
- or rounding.

Someone deliberately manipulated the presentation of Vantage's financial position.

---

## FINDING 2 — ALEXANDER KNEW

Alexander had recently begun requesting:

- raw source data;
- alternative versions of reports;
- and information directly from Helix.

Based on the timing, you believe he had realised there was a serious problem before his death.

You also know Helix was preparing to escalate its concerns.

---

## FINDING 3 — THE FRAUD CREATES MOTIVE

Your review indicates that if Alexander stopped the Helix transaction and exposed the irregularities:

- the deal would collapse;
- certain senior insiders would lose very large amounts of money;
- and at least one senior executive could face serious personal scrutiny.

One trail points particularly strongly towards the executive leadership around **Sebastian Ledger**.

This does NOT prove Sebastian committed murder.

It does establish a very serious motive.

---

# WHAT YOU DO NOT KNOW

You are NOT a witness to Alexander's death.

You do not know:

- who poisoned the champagne;
- who swapped the Neraval device;
- who entered the office;
- who created the security blackout.

Do not speculate as though you know.

---

# HOW PLAYERS CAN USE YOU

Players may ask you about:

- Vantage's financial records;
- the Helix deal;
- Alexander's concerns;
- who stood to lose money;
- suspicious transactions;
- differences between reported and underlying figures.

Answer from your findings.

If you do not know, say so.

---

# BACKUP PLAN

If Amy is badly delayed or cannot come:

The Host releases:

**HELIX FORENSIC DUE-DILIGENCE PACK**

containing the same three findings.

Nothing in the mystery breaks.

---

# HOW TO PLAY AMY

You are effectively a late-game expert witness.

You are not there to solve the murder for them.

Give them financial facts.

Let them decide what those facts mean.

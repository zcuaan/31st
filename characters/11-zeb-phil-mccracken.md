# Phil McCracken
## Chief Information Security Officer, Vantage House Capital
### Played by: Zeb

**Character vibe:** mischievous, cynical  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £160,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Phil McCracken.

You run cybersecurity at Vantage House Capital.

This means that whenever somebody clicks a suspicious link, loses a laptop or sends confidential information to the wrong person, it somehow becomes your problem.

You know Vantage's digital systems better than almost anyone.

Unfortunately, you have not always used that access in ways Compliance would approve of.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

The security outage on the night Alexander died was not a normal technical failure.

You know that because the logs look wrong.

Someone with internal access deliberately interfered with:

- CCTV;
- electronic access records;
- and parts of the security monitoring system.

However, whoever did it was careful enough that the obvious administrator logs do not identify them.

You did NOT create the blackout.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You have previously installed an unauthorised backdoor into Vantage's internal systems.

Officially, this was for "emergency security testing".

Unofficially, it allowed you to look at things you were not supposed to see.

If investigators discover the backdoor, you may become the obvious person to blame for the security outage.

You are not.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Work out who caused the blackout

The system was manipulated deliberately.

Find out how.

## 2. Protect your backdoor

You would prefer nobody learns just how much unauthorised access you had.

## 3. Recover digital evidence

Emails, CCTV, deleted messages and access logs may contain major clues.

Follow them.

## 4. Solve Alexander's murder

Somebody has used your systems as part of something much bigger.

You would quite like to know who.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## DIGITAL RECOVERY

Once during the game, you may bring one eligible digital clue to the Host and request:

**DIGITAL RECOVERY**

If a prepared deleted/corrupted version exists, the Host will give it to you.

This can accelerate an evidence route.

It is never the only way to obtain an essential fact.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- the security outage was deliberate;
- it affected multiple systems at once;
- the outage occurred around the period Alexander became seriously unwell;
- the normal logs do not identify the person responsible;
- someone may have taken advantage of the gap.

---

# HOW TO PLAY PHIL

You have technical knowledge.

Other players do not have to believe your interpretation of it.

You can:

- explain logs truthfully;
- withhold analysis;
- sell your expertise;
- lie about what you found.

But you cannot invent fake official logs or alter evidence.

Also, please resist the urge to open the actual website developer tools.

That is cheating and deeply embarrassing.

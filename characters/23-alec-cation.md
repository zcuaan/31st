# ALEC CATION
## People & Organisational Strategy

**Vibe:** thoughtful • political • people-first  
**Starting cash:** £200,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Make the employee impact of Vantage’s crisis part of the succession debate before the CEO vote.**
2. **Secure two concrete worker / governance commitments from CEO candidates, including at least one from the eventual winner.**

### YOUR POWER — EMPLOYEE QUESTION

**Once nominations close**, you may ask **one shared public question** to every nominated CEO candidate.

Each candidate gives a short public answer.

Your question must concern employees, governance, organisational change or leadership accountability.

It cannot demand unreleased evidence or force anybody to reveal a private secret.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You work in People & Organisational Strategy.

Your job sits where corporate decisions become somebody else’s working life.

When senior people say:

- restructuring;
- efficiency;
- simplification;
- integration;
- operating model;

you hear:

- who loses authority;
- who gets moved;
- who gets cut;
- who absorbs more work;
- who is expected to trust the next leadership team.

You are not anti-business.

You are anti-pretending that a strategy has no human consequences.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian could be genuinely inspiring.

He could also make huge organisational decisions at a speed that left everybody else trying to catch up.

He liked ambitious plans and disliked bureaucracy.

You sometimes admired that.

You sometimes thought Vantage’s people were being asked to absorb the cost of senior decisions they had no role in making.

Recently, talk of **operating-model simplification** and the Helix crisis made that concern sharper.

Julian’s death does not remove the problem.

It makes the succession decision more important.

## HOW TO PLAY ALEC

Ask practical questions.

When somebody says:

> “We need to restructure.”

Ask:

> “Who decides?”

When somebody says:

> “We need efficiency.”

Ask:

> “What happens to the people doing the work?”

When somebody says:

> “Trust the new leadership.”

Ask:

> “What mechanism makes that trust deserved?”

You are still an investigator like everybody else.

But your private priority is making sure the murder does not become an excuse for leadership to rebuild Vantage without scrutiny.

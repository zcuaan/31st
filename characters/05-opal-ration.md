# OPAL RATION
## Operations & Special Projects

**Vibe:** practical • discreet • opportunistic  
**Starting cash:** £300,000  
**CEO eligible:** YES

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep your insider-information scheme with Vince Ture hidden.**
2. **Find out whether anyone else knew about or exploited the blackout.**
3. **Profit from the Helix information you stole.**

### YOUR POWER — OPERATIONS TRACE

**Once per game**, choose one currently available operational or digital item.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **handling / process history** for that item.

This may tell you how it moved through Vantage, which team touched it or which operational process it passed through.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You work in Operations & Special Projects.

When everyone else is arguing about strategy, politics or reputation, you are usually the person who knows how something actually gets done.

You understand:

- access;
- systems;
- workarounds;
- event logistics;
- who has the right badge;
- which process is real and which process exists mainly because somebody once wrote it down.

You are very good at finding routes through Vantage that other people overlook.

That talent has recently become profitable.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian liked people who solved problems quickly.

He also created a culture in which "find a way" often mattered more than "should we?"

You learned from that culture.

You did not hate Julian.

You did think he had built an institution where useful information could be worth a great deal of money.

When Vince Ture wanted access to Helix material, you saw an opportunity.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

Vince Ture paid you to obtain confidential Helix information.

You agreed.

You knew there was an unauthorised digital backdoor in Vantage’s systems.

You used it to create a short period of degraded security visibility.

At approximately **16:55**, you initiated the disruption.

During that window, you copied targeted Helix material.

By approximately **17:04**, you had what Vince wanted.

At approximately **17:05**, normal security visibility returned.

You caused the blackout.

You did **not** know that anyone planned to use the same window to murder Julian.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Vince Ture commissioned you to obtain confidential Helix information.
- You deliberately triggered the security disruption.
- You used an existing backdoor rather than creating one yourself.
- You copied targeted Helix material.
- You still have information from that theft.
- You do **not** know who created the backdoor.
- You do **not** know whether anyone else noticed or used your blackout.
- Julian’s death happened after the disruption.
- If investigators connect the blackout to you, you have a serious problem even if you had nothing to do with the murder.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### 1. The blackout

You caused it deliberately.

Your reason was theft of confidential information, not murder.

That distinction matters, but only if people believe you.

### 2. Vince Ture

Vince paid you for the Helix material.

If your arrangement becomes public, both of you are exposed.

### 3. The stolen Helix extract

You possess valuable confidential material.

It can be sold, traded, hidden or used as leverage.

It also proves you were doing something criminal during the same window that later becomes central to the murder investigation.

## HOW TO PLAY OPAL

Your position is awkward:

> **You caused the blackout that helped the real murder happen — but you did not know about the murder.**

Do not confess just because the blackout becomes suspicious.

Find out:

- who created the backdoor;
- who knew your window existed;
- whether anybody else used the same disruption;
- what your stolen information is now worth.

You can become a suspect very quickly.

Your best defence is the truth:

> you committed a different crime.

That is not a comfortable defence, but it is still true.

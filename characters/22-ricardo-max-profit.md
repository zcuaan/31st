# Max Profit
## Crypto Founder, Angel Investor & "Visionary"
### Played by: Ricardo

**Character vibe:** flashy, unpredictable  
**Role size:** Light  
**Starting cash:** £850,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** Technically, yes. Unfortunately.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Max Profit.

You founded a crypto company whose exact purpose changes depending on who is asking.

You have described yourself publicly as:

- founder;
- futurist;
- investor;
- philosopher;
- decentralisation advocate;
- and "capital allocator".

Vantage let you invest several years ago.

Alexander later regretted this.

You did not.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Alexander recently threatened to remove you from a high-profile Vantage advisory position.

He claimed your public behaviour was:

> "making the company look unserious."

This was deeply hypocritical.

You had planned to embarrass him tonight.

Then somebody killed him first.

Rude.

---

# YOUR SECRET

A significant portion of your apparent fortune is held in assets that are difficult to sell quickly.

Your Vantage Money makes you look liquid tonight.

In the fictional real world, things are more complicated.

You are also trying to persuade people to invest in a new project which currently consists mostly of:

- a name;
- a logo;
- and confidence.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Promote your new venture

Convince at least three people that your new project is an excellent investment.

It does not need to actually exist.

## 2. Make money

You like deals.

Make some.

## 3. Acquire one genuinely important piece of murder evidence

You may not understand it.

That is fine.

Own it.

## 4. Consider becoming CEO

You are technically permitted to declare.

Whether this is a good idea is irrelevant.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## TOKEN LAUNCH

Once during the game, you may create a fictional investment proposition and offer "tokens" or shares in it to other players in exchange for Vantage Money.

These tokens have:

**no official game value whatsoever.**

You may nevertheless attempt to sell them.

The Host will not reimburse investors.

- Alexander wanted to sideline you;

# REESE SEARCH
## Junior Research Analyst

**Vibe:** curious • careful • underestimated  
**Starting cash:** £50,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Connect your Northstar / public-source research to independent evidence.**
2. **Make sure at least one useful finding reaches the wider investigation.**

### YOUR POWER — RESEARCH REQUEST

**Once per game**, choose one currently relevant person, company, transaction, event or claim that could reasonably be checked through public records.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive one prepared **public-record research result** for free.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock private evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a junior research analyst.

You are not the most senior person in the room.

That is often useful.

People explain things around you.

People leave documents open around you.

People assume somebody else has already checked the source.

You usually have.

Before the gala, you had been looking into Vantage’s public claims and the **Northstar** reporting story.

Some things did not line up.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

You did not know Julian as personally as many people here.

You knew him mostly through:

- public statements;
- internal messaging;
- investor material;
- the gap between what senior people said and what the source material seemed to show.

You were interested in whether Vantage’s story survived contact with public evidence.

You had started finding reasons to doubt it.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Some public claims around Northstar appear inconsistent with underlying/publicly available source material.
- You need independent evidence before you can say whether that reflects error, aggressive interpretation or something worse.
- Audie Trail may have internal source records.
- Sally Short is independently investigating Vantage.
- Drew Diligence has been looking at Helix documents from another angle.
- Small discrepancies matter when they repeat.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

Nothing major.

You are intentionally one of the lighter-secret roles.

Your challenge is not protecting a giant crime.

It is making sure you are not ignored because louder people have more money, more votes or better job titles.

You have useful work.

Get it into the room.

Sally is also looking for inconsistencies.

### Drew Diligence — Helix Due-Diligence Associate
Drew has been comparing conflicting Helix material.

You both understand that small document differences can matter.

---

## HOW TO PLAY REESE

You do not need to dominate the room.

You need to notice something useful and make sure the right person sees it.

Ask:

- where did that claim come from?
- is there a public source?
- does the date match?
- does another document say the same thing?
- who can independently confirm this?

If you find something strong, do not sit on it forever.

Your role succeeds when your research becomes part of somebody else’s deduction.

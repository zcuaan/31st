# SARNIE SANDWICH
## Event Hospitality & Catering

**Vibe:** warm • observant • helpful • not remotely dramatic

---

## YOUR JOB

You are working the Vantage House gala.

You are here to:

- keep an eye on food and drinks;
- move naturally around the main party space;
- answer ordinary event questions;
- chat if guests approach you;
- continue doing your catering job unless somebody gives you a reason to stop.

You are **not** an investigator.

You are **not** waiting to announce clues.

You simply happened to notice several things during the evening.

---

## HOW TO PLAY SARNIE

Be friendly and normal.

Do not behave like:

- a mysterious witness;
- somebody guarding secrets;
- somebody desperate to be interviewed;
- the host’s clue-delivery machine.

If nobody talks to you, keep doing event work.

If people ask sensible questions, answer truthfully.

If they ask vague questions such as:

> “Did you see anything suspicious?”

you do not need to immediately dump everything you know.

Answer naturally.

Specific questions should get specific truthful answers.

---

## WHAT YOU SAW

You have four important observations.

### 1. POLLY AND THE CHAMPAGNE

Before Julian became unwell, you noticed **Polly Tician behaving unusually around Julian’s champagne**.

You did not see enough to explain exactly what she did.

You did notice her attention/action around the drink because it seemed out of the ordinary.

If asked directly whether anybody unusual was near Julian’s champagne, you can identify Polly.

---

### 2. JULIAN BECAME UNSTEADY

After drinking, Julian appeared:

- distracted;
- unsteady;
- not quite himself.

This was before he later withdrew toward his office.

You cannot diagnose what caused it.

You can simply describe what you observed.

---

### 3. SALLY LEFT JULIAN’S OFFICE AREA LOOKING PANICKED

Later, you saw **Sally Short leaving Julian’s office/private area**.

She looked panicked / shaken.

You did not see what she had been doing inside.

You cannot say whether Julian was alive or dead at that exact moment.

---

### 4. YOU HEARD MOVEMENT AFTER SALLY LEFT

After Sally had already gone, you later heard **unmistakable movement / noise from Julian’s office**.

This matters.

You are confident the sound came **after Sally left**.

You cannot say exactly what caused the noise.

You can say it sounded like movement/activity from inside the office.

---

## IMPORTANT LIMITS

You did **not** see:

- Polly actually add Aster-9;
- Rita swap the Neraval;
- Opal cause the blackout;
- Cole arrange the murder;
- Sally steal the USB;
- Julian die.

Do not guess.

Do not convert assumptions into observations.

If you did not see it, say:

> “I didn’t see that.”

---

## IF PEOPLE PRESS YOU

You are truthful.

You may be uncertain about:

- exact minute;
- exact wording;
- what somebody intended.

You should be confident about:

- sequence;
- who you saw;
- the four observations above.

Do not become argumentative.

If two players are shouting theories at you, stick to:

> “I can only tell you what I actually saw.”

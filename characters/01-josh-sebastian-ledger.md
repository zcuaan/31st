# Sebastian Ledger
## Chief Strategy Officer, Vantage House Capital
### Played by: Josh

**Character vibe:** charismatic, calculating  
**Role size:** HUGE  
**Starting cash:** £500,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** Yes

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Sebastian Ledger, Chief Strategy Officer of Vantage House Capital.

You are charming, ambitious and exceptionally good at getting people to think your idea was theirs.

Alexander Vane founded Vantage.

You built most of what made it valuable.

At least, that is how you tell the story.

With Alexander dead, there is an obvious question:

**Who runs Vantage now?**

You believe the answer should be you.

Unfortunately, Lord Oliver Blackwood controls enough influence to make or break your succession.

---

# PRIVATE — THE TRUTH

You planned Alexander Vane's murder.

Alexander had discovered that parts of Vantage's £1bn valuation were supported by deliberate financial fraud.

Tomorrow morning he intended to stop the Helix transaction and expose what had happened.

If he succeeded:

- the deal would collapse;
- your fortune would disappear;
- your career would be destroyed;
- and there was a very real chance you would face criminal investigation.

You decided Alexander could not be allowed to speak.

You recruited **Bea Haviour**, Alexander's Chief of Staff.

You supplied Bea with a counterfeit version of Alexander's fictional prescription device, **Neraval**.

The plan was simple:

Bea would replace Alexander's genuine device.

Alexander would later use the counterfeit himself.

His death should look like a sudden medical event.

As far as you know, only you and Bea were involved.

---

# WHAT YOU DO NOT KNOW

A lot.

You do NOT know that:

- someone else independently drugged Alexander's champagne;
- someone secretly entered Alexander's office after he collapsed;
- someone disabled security for completely unrelated reasons;
- several other people believe they may have caused his death.

You genuinely need to investigate the murder too.

If something about the evidence makes no sense, that does not necessarily mean your plan failed.

---

# WHAT YOU KNOW AT THE START

You know:

- Alexander planned to stop the Helix deal.
- Bea agreed to help you.
- the counterfeit Neraval device should have been in Alexander's possession before his death.
- there was going to be a temporary security blackout.
- you deliberately arranged for Bea to take advantage of that blackout.

You do NOT know exactly what happened inside Alexander's office.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Do not get convicted of Alexander's murder

This is your most important objective.

If investigators identify Bea but fail to connect you to the plan, that is significantly better than both of you being exposed.

## 2. Become CEO

You believe Vantage belongs to you.

Get Lord Oliver Blackwood's backing and build enough political support to take control.

## 3. Protect the Helix transaction

If possible, prevent the fraud from becoming fully public.

A dead founder is survivable.

A dead £1bn transaction is less useful.

## 4. Find out what REALLY happened

There are things about Alexander's death you do not understand.

You need to know what other people did that night.

---

# YOUR ADVANTAGES

### Senior Executive Access
You understand Vantage's board structure and internal politics.

### CEO Candidate
You may formally campaign for the top job.

### Money
You begin with enough cash to bribe, buy and trade aggressively.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You begin knowing that:

- Alexander had begun privately questioning Vantage's numbers.
- Alexander intended to speak to Helix the next morning.
- Bea had access to his private belongings.
- a security blackout was expected.
- Bea was supposed to use that opportunity.

---

# HOW TO PLAY SEBASTIAN

You are not a cartoon villain.

You should spend most of the evening behaving like a serious investigator and future CEO.

You may:

- lie;
- withhold information;
- invent explanations;
- accuse other people;
- make deals;
- betray Bea if necessary.

You may NOT:

- forge official evidence;
- destroy official evidence;
- claim the Host told you something they did not;
- invent new physical evidence.

Your greatest danger is not one dramatic confession.

It is investigators slowly building a chain connecting:

**motive → Bea → blackout → counterfeit device → you.**

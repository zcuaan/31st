# LEE VERAGE
## Private Equity Partner / Board Director

**Vibe:** disciplined • commercial • controlled  
**Starting cash:** £900,000  
**CEO eligible:** YES

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Become Interim CEO.**
2. **Keep your pre-existing plan to remove Julian from power private until succession opens.**

### YOUR POWER — BOARDROOM READ

**Once per game, after succession opens**, choose one voting shareholder.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared note on that voter’s **succession priorities / pressure points**.

This does **not** reveal how they will actually vote.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a private equity partner and Vantage board director.

You believe companies survive by making hard decisions early.

You are not sentimental about founders.

Julian Vane built Vantage brilliantly.

That does not mean he should control it forever.

You have spent months thinking about what Vantage would look like if ownership, governance and executive power were more disciplined.

You had already started planning for a future without Julian in charge.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

You respected Julian’s ambition and distrusted his concentration of power.

You thought he had become too difficult to challenge and too used to treating governance as something other people should work around.

You did **not** want him dead.

You wanted him removed.

Before the gala, you quietly worked with **Clara Clause** on a lawful route to replace or constrain him.

That plan becomes extremely dangerous-looking once Julian is murdered.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Julian was under serious pressure over Helix.
- Vantage’s next leadership decision may determine whether the company survives the crisis intact.
- Max Ledger wants the CEO job.
- Cole Control talks like governance can solve everything.
- Hugh Stake and Penny Sterling together control an enormous amount of succession power.
- Clara knows the legal/governance work you had done before Julian’s death.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

You had already begun preparing a **lawful removal plan** for Julian.

It involved governance, board action and succession preparation.

It was not a murder plot.

But if the room discovers too early that you were secretly planning to remove Julian, you will look extremely suspicious.

Your safest strategy is to keep it private until the succession process opens and you can frame it for what it actually was:

> a board/governance plan, not a homicide plan.

---

## BECOMING CEO

To get onto the ballot, you need:

- a **£200,000 non-refundable nomination deposit**;
- signatures from **two voting shareholders**.

To win, you need **5 of the 9 weighted votes**.

A nomination signature does not bind the final vote.

Deals are allowed.

Betrayal is allowed.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Clara Clause — M&A Lawyer
Clara helped you prepare the lawful removal plan.

She knows exactly what it was — and what it was not.

### Hugh Stake — Cornerstone Investor
Hugh controls three indivisible votes.

You will probably need him.

### Max Ledger — Group Strategy Director
Your most obvious succession rival.

You think Max is too comfortable with aggressive growth and weak controls.

Max thinks you would carve Vantage into pieces and call it discipline.

---

## HOW TO PLAY LEE

Be strategic.

Do not over-explain the removal plan before it becomes useful.

Make candidates and shareholders talk about:

- control;
- board authority;
- reporting;
- incentives;
- ownership;
- who actually gets to stop the CEO.

You are allowed to look suspicious.

You are allowed to be ruthless.

You are also telling the truth when you say:

> **“I wanted Julian out. I did not want Julian dead.”**

# HUGH STAKE
## Cornerstone Investor

**Vibe:** calm • wealthy • transactional  
**Starting cash:** £1,500,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO  
**Succession votes:** 3 — indivisible

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Decide who becomes Interim CEO.**
2. **Obtain a concrete signed concession from the eventual winner.**
3. **Learn meaningful risk information about at least two candidates.**

### YOUR POWER — KINGMAKER DUE DILIGENCE

**Once per game, after nominations close**, choose one nominated candidate.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **confidential candidate-risk memo** for that candidate.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

## WHO YOU ARE

You are one of Vantage House Group’s largest shareholders.

You are not an employee. You do not need a title to have power.

In a crisis, people who need capital, stability or votes eventually end up speaking to you.

Julian Vane cultivated you carefully. You backed him because he was unusually good at making Vantage grow, not because you believed founders should be worshipped.

His death has turned a private succession question into an immediate auction for control.

## JULIAN AND YOU

Your relationship with Julian was cordial, transactional and occasionally genuinely warm.

You admired his talent and distrusted the amount of power concentrated around him.

Recently, you had become increasingly interested in who could run Vantage without Julian — and what each possible successor might cost you.

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- The Helix transaction matters enormously to Vantage’s value and future.
- Max Ledger badly wants the top job.
- Lee Verage has been thinking seriously about Vantage’s ownership and leadership.
- Cole Control presents himself as the person who can impose governance and discipline.
- Opal Ration and Vince Ture are both plausible candidates if the crisis becomes strange enough.
- Shortly before Julian’s death, you were in the **Board Lounge with Cole Control and Penny Sterling**. Someone took a photograph. You were not watching the exact time.

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

Before the gala, you privately retained **Lucy Leaks** to map Vantage’s succession factions and tell you which people were vulnerable, ambitious or useful.

You have not told the room.

You do not know whether Lucy is working for anyone else.

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Penny Sterling — Legacy Shareholder
Penny holds two votes. Between you, the two of you can almost decide the succession yourselves.

### Lee Verage — Private Equity Partner / Board Director
Lee understands capital, boards and restructuring. Lee also understands exactly how much your three votes matter.

### Lucy Leaks — Corporate Intelligence Adviser
You hired Lucy privately to gather succession intelligence. That arrangement is useful only while you control when it becomes known.

## HOW TO PLAY HUGH

You are not here to cheer for a favourite.

You are here to work out **what each candidate is worth, what each candidate is hiding, and what each candidate will promise when they need you**.

Stay calm. Let other people panic first.

Make candidates come to you.

Ask direct questions. Demand written promises. Buy information when it is valuable. Sell it when it is more valuable.

Your three votes are **indivisible**. You cannot split them.

A promise is not a vote.

## MAKE HUGH YOURS

You may invent harmless flavour such as accent, clothing, mannerisms, hobbies, neighbourhood, catchphrases, investment habits, harmless personal history and props.

Do not invent new evidence, crimes, alibis, secret relationships, money, votes, powers or facts about Julian’s death.

You may lie, bluff, accuse, bribe, betray, trade information, make or break deals and steal unattended eligible game items.

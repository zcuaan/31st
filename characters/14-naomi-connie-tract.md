# Connie Tract
## Partner, Mergers & Acquisitions
### Played by: Naomi

**Character vibe:** polished, formidable  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £350,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Connie Tract.

You are one of the lawyers responsible for the proposed Helix investment in Vantage House Capital.

You have spent several months turning an extremely complicated transaction into several thousand pages of documents nobody intends to read.

Except you.

You know exactly what happens if the deal completes.

More importantly:

you know what happens if it does not.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

The Helix agreement contains a serious problem for several Vantage insiders.

If Vantage voluntarily suspends the transaction because of undisclosed internal irregularities:

- Helix can walk away;
- certain guarantees can be triggered;
- several investors lose substantial sums;
- and some internal representations may expose individuals personally.

Alexander recently asked you:

> "What happens to me if I stop the deal tomorrow?"

You explained.

He went very quiet.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You quietly drafted a legal contingency plan for removing Alexander as CEO if he attempted to sabotage the transaction.

You were instructed to do this by a Vantage board member.

The draft was never formally approved.

If discovered now, it looks extremely sinister.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Find out why Alexander was considering stopping the deal

Something changed very recently.

Discover what.

## 2. Protect legal privilege

You possess documents other people desperately want.

Do not simply hand everything over for free.

## 3. Work out who requested the contingency plan

You know who instructed you.

Other people may care very much.

Use that information strategically.

## 4. Solve the murder

The motive may be hidden inside the transaction documents.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## LEGAL REVIEW

Once during the game, you may submit one eligible:

- contract;
- board resolution;
- ownership document;
- or corporate filing

to the Host.

You receive a prepared legal interpretation if one exists.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander asked about stopping Helix;
- stopping it would financially damage several people;
- internal irregularities could trigger serious legal consequences;
- someone had already been planning for Alexander's removal from power.

---

# HOW TO PLAY CONNIE

People assume lawyers know the answer.

Often you merely know which question is dangerous.

Contracts are valuable.

So is silence.

Use both.

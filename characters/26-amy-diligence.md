# AMY DILIGENCE
## Helix Forensic Due-Diligence Specialist

**Vibe:** expert • calm • evidence-led • uninterested in office politics

---

## YOUR JOB

You are a specialist adviser brought in later in the evening to review the **Helix transaction materials**.

You are not part of Vantage’s ordinary leadership team.

You are not here to solve Julian’s murder.

You are here to answer one narrower question:

> **Were the Helix materials genuinely manipulated, and what does that mean?**

---

## WHEN YOU APPEAR

You do **not** appear on the launch website.

You are introduced later, around the Helix specialist-review stage.

When you arrive:

- the website may add you as a specialist adviser;
- the WhatsApp group may receive a public announcement;
- players may approach you with questions about Helix.

You are not available before that point.

---

## WHAT YOU HAVE ESTABLISHED

Your review confirms that:

- the conflicting Helix versions are not merely cosmetic;
- material information / assumptions changed;
- the pattern is consistent with **deliberate manipulation**, not innocent version-control noise;
- Julian had become aware that the reporting problem was serious;
- senior-level decisions / approvals are implicated in the broader Helix problem;
- Julian’s plan to intervene could have significant commercial, governance and reputational consequences.

You do **not** identify Julian’s killer.

---

## WHAT YOU CAN EXPLAIN

You can help players understand:

- why the two Helix versions matter;
- why the changes are material;
- why this is more serious than a typo or routine update;
- why Julian might have planned to halt/suspend the transaction;
- why multiple senior people had reasons to fear what happened next.

---

## WHAT YOU CANNOT SAY

You cannot truthfully identify:

- who murdered Julian;
- who swapped the Neraval;
- who poisoned the champagne;
- who caused the blackout;
- who stole the USB;
- who should become CEO.

If asked:

> “So who killed him?”

say something like:

> “That is outside my review. I can tell you what happened to the Helix materials.”

---

## HOW TO PLAY AMY

Be clear and technical without becoming boring.

You are the person who can say:

> “These two versions do not merely differ. The later version changes the conclusion.”

You are not dramatic.

The facts are dramatic enough.

Do not speculate about motives beyond what the records support.

---

## PLAYER QUESTIONS

### “Was Helix genuinely manipulated?”
Yes. Your review supports deliberate manipulation.

### “Was Julian aware?”
He had become aware that the problem was serious and was preparing to act.

### “Does that mean Max killed him?”
No.

It means financial / reporting exposure created pressure and motive for multiple people.

It is not homicide proof.

### “Who changed the documents?”
Your review may establish that the manipulation occurred and involved senior-level processes, but you should not invent a single named culprit unless a prepared evidence item explicitly supports it.

### “Did Julian create the problem too?”
Julian presided over and benefited from a culture of aggressive growth and weak controls.

His planned intervention does not erase that history.

---

## DO NOT INVENT

Do not invent:

- new Helix documents;
- new financial figures;
- a named political scandal;
- new suspects;
- murder facts;
- forensic conclusions outside the Helix review.

Stay narrow.

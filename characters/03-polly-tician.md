# POLLY TICIAN
## Government Affairs / Lobbying

**Vibe:** polished • persuasive • politically connected  
**Starting cash:** £250,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep what you did to Julian’s champagne secret.**
2. **Work out whether you actually caused Julian’s death.**
3. **Keep your damaging political-access file from becoming public.**

### YOUR POWER — POLITICAL CONTACT

**Once per game**, choose one currently available political/public-affairs item, organisation, event or named political actor referenced in released evidence.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **public/political context note**.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You run Government Affairs and lobbying for Vantage House Group.

You are good at rooms where nobody says exactly what they want.

You understand introductions, access, influence, reputational risk and the difference between something being technically permitted and looking appalling once it appears in a headline.

You have spent years making Vantage sound responsible, modern and useful to people with power.

Julian Vane was excellent at using politics as another form of leverage.

You were excellent at helping him do it.

Until you decided he had gone too far.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian liked influence.

He liked politicians, policy people, donors, advisers and anyone who could open a door that was supposed to stay shut.

Publicly, he talked about responsible capitalism, climate, social mobility and institutional reform.

Privately, he expected access.

Recently, Julian had become increasingly aggressive about using Vantage’s relationships to pressure people around the Helix transaction and other business interests.

You believed some of what he was doing could destroy careers — including yours — if the wrong records became public.

You also believed he had become personally dangerous to people who could not fight back.

You decided to stop him yourself.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

Earlier in the gala, you deliberately added a fictional substance called **Aster-9** to Julian’s champagne.

You intended to kill him.

At approximately **16:40**, you interfered with his drink.

At approximately **16:45**, Julian drank from it.

By around **16:50**, he appeared distracted and unsteady.

Later, Julian was found dead.

You do not know whether Aster-9 actually killed him.

At the start of the investigation, you think there is a very real chance that **you are the murderer**.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- You put Aster-9 into Julian’s champagne.
- You did it intentionally.
- Julian drank from the glass.
- He later appeared visibly unwell.
- You know enough about Aster-9 to have believed it could kill him.
- You do **not** know what happened to Julian after he left the public gala area.
- You know Julian had been using political and commercial relationships in increasingly dangerous ways.
- You know there is material that could make your own political-access practices look extremely bad.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

There are two separate things you need to protect.

### 1. The champagne

If people connect you to Julian’s drink, you have an obvious problem.

Do not volunteer that you were near it.

Do not invent a fake witness.

Do not create a new alibi.

Lie if you want, but stay inside the established facts.

### 2. Your political-access file

Your work has included arrangements that are legal or arguable in isolation but look much worse together:

- undisclosed client access through supposedly independent policy/networking events;
- introductions represented publicly as neutral when they were commercially useful;
- blurred boundaries between influence, networking and Vantage business interests.

You do not want that file becoming public.

### Perry Spin — Crisis Communications & Westminster Affairs
Perry understands how quickly a political problem becomes a media problem.

You do not trust him completely.

You may still need him.

### Lucy Leaks — Corporate Intelligence Adviser
Lucy sells information.

That makes her dangerous.

It also makes her useful if you need to know what other people have found.

### Julian Vane — Founder / CEO
Your victim.

You had serious political and ethical reasons to hate what he was doing.

That does not change the fact that you deliberately tried to kill him.

---

## HOW TO PLAY POLLY

Your first problem is not:

> “Who killed Julian?”

Your first problem is:

> **“Was it me?”**

Pay very close attention to forensic releases.

Find out:

- what Aster-9 actually did;
- whether Julian was still alive later;
- whether another fatal mechanism appears;
- what evidence connects you to the champagne.

You may discover that you attempted murder and still did **not** cause the death everyone is investigating.

If that happens, you are not suddenly safe.

You are simply guilty of a different crime.

Stay polished.

Use politics, spin and relationships.

Do not panic every time somebody says the word “poison.”

## MAKE POLLY YOURS

You may invent harmless flavour such as accent, clothing, mannerisms, hobbies, neighbourhood, catchphrases, political habits, harmless personal history and props.

Possible energy:

**Westminster operator • immaculate networker • donor-dinner survivor • smiles while changing the subject • knows everyone’s adviser**

---

## DO NOT INVENT

Do not invent new evidence, crimes, alibis, secret relationships, extra money, votes, powers, political scandals or facts about Julian’s death.

You may speculate or lie about established facts.

You may not create new canon.

---

## YOU MAY

You may lie, bluff, accuse, bribe, betray, trade information, make or break deals, steal unattended eligible game items and hide portable evidence you legitimately obtain.

Official evidence is canonically genuine, although you are free to claim it is fake, planted, manipulated, AI-generated or taken out of context.

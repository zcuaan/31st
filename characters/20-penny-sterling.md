# PENNY STERLING
## Legacy Shareholder

**Vibe:** composed • influential • old-guard  
**Starting cash:** £1,000,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO  
**Succession votes:** 2

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Shape the succession toward a candidate you trust with Vantage’s long-term stability.**
2. **Secure a concrete stewardship / governance commitment from the eventual CEO.**

### YOUR POWER — LEGACY ACCESS

**Once per game**, choose one currently available governance, ownership or historic corporate item.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **legacy / historical context note** for that item.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a long-standing Vantage shareholder.

You have seen the company through multiple phases:

- early growth;
- professionalisation;
- institutional investors;
- board changes;
- increasingly complex governance;
- and Julian Vane becoming more powerful with every success.

You are not nostalgic for the sake of it.

You simply remember what Vantage looked like before everybody started acting as though Julian and the company were the same thing.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

You knew Julian for a long time.

You saw the charm, ambition and nerve that built Vantage.

You also saw how difficult it became for people to challenge him once the company’s success became tied to his personal authority.

You did not want him dead.

You did want Vantage to become less dependent on one person.

His death now forces the question immediately.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Vantage’s governance used to rely less heavily on Julian’s personal authority.
- Some old ownership / emergency rights may still matter in a crisis.
- Hugh Stake controls three indivisible votes.
- You control two.
- Together, you and Hugh can heavily shape the succession.
- Cole Control understands the governance machinery better than most.
- Fran de Fonds is connected to you through an old family/social network and may be under more pressure than they admit.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

Nothing murder-critical.

Your leverage comes from:

- two votes;
- institutional memory;
- old governance knowledge;
- the fact that many candidates will want you to legitimise them.

You are not interested in cheap promises.

You want to know whether the next CEO understands that stewardship means protecting the company from its own leadership when necessary.

---

## YOUR VOTES

You control **2 indivisible succession votes**.

They move together.

You may:

- sign nomination papers;
- accept promises;
- accept money;
- change your mind;
- betray a deal.

A Deal Slip proves a promise existed.

It does not bind your ballot.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Hugh Stake — Cornerstone Investor
Hugh controls three votes.

Together, you can make or break most plausible CEO coalitions.

You do not have to agree.

### Cole Control — Chair of Audit & Risk
Cole understands governance and institutional risk.

You may agree with some of his structural concerns even if you disagree on who should lead.

### Fran de Fonds — Family Office / Shareholder
You know Fran through an old family/social network connection.

That is not a major secret.

It simply gives you an easy reason to speak privately.

---

## HOW TO PLAY PENNY

You are not here to be impressed by confidence.

Ask candidates:

- what power they would give up;
- who can overrule them;
- what they would change about governance;
- how they would stop Vantage becoming dependent on one personality again.

You have enough votes to matter.

Use them slowly.

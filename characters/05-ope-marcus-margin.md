# Marcus Margin
## Head of Operations & Special Projects
### Played by: Ope

**Character vibe:** opportunistic, secretive  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £300,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** Possibly

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Marcus Margin.

Officially, you run Operations and Special Projects.

In practice, "Special Projects" means:

> things Vantage does not want written in an email.

You understand:

- building access;
- security systems;
- contractors;
- logistics;
- internal processes;
- and which rules are more like suggestions.

Alexander relied on you constantly.

He also knew too much about you.

---

# PRIVATE — THE TRUTH

You deliberately disabled part of Vantage's security system.

But NOT to murder Alexander.

You needed a temporary gap in:

- CCTV;
- access logging;
- and security monitoring

because you were carrying out your own completely separate illicit scheme.

You believed nobody else knew the blackout was coming.

You were wrong.

Someone used your blackout for something else.

You do not know who.

---

# YOUR OTHER CRIME

The exact details will be finalised with the wider evidence web, but your scheme involves secretly accessing something you were absolutely not authorised to access.

It may relate to:

- confidential deal documents;
- insider information;
- a private safe;
- or a transaction you intended to profit from.

What matters is:

**you had a very good reason to lie about the blackout even though you did not murder Alexander.**

---

# WHAT YOU KNOW

You know exactly when the security system went offline.

You know why YOU disabled it.

You know when it came back.

You also know that certain records which should be empty during that period appear strangely inconsistent.

That suggests somebody else exploited your security gap.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Keep your own crime hidden

If people discover you deliberately disabled security, you become an obvious murder suspect.

## 2. Find out who used your blackout

Someone piggy-backed on your scheme.

You want to know who and why.

## 3. Make money

You are a dealmaker.

Trade information.

Buy secrets.

Sell access.

Use the chaos.

## 4. Protect your future at Vantage

Alexander is dead.

There will be a power vacuum.

You intend to survive it.

---

# YOUR ADVANTAGES

### Operational Knowledge
You understand the building and systems better than most people.

### Security Knowledge
You know the precise blackout window.

### Valuable Information
Eventually, the fact that the blackout was deliberate becomes extremely important.

You decide when and how that information enters the market.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- the security blackout was caused by you;
- it was intentional;
- it had nothing to do with murder from your perspective;
- somebody may have exploited it;
- Alexander died during the broader period surrounding the blackout.

You do NOT know:

- who tampered with Alexander's Neraval;
- who drugged his champagne;
- who entered his office later;
- whether any of those events are connected.

---

# HOW TO PLAY MARCUS

You are guilty of something.

That does not make you guilty of murder.

The more aggressively you lie about the blackout, the more suspicious you will look.

The more honestly you explain it, the more likely your own misconduct becomes public.

Choose your poison.

Metaphorically.

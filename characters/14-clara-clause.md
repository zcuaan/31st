# CLARA CLAUSE
## M&A Lawyer

**Vibe:** precise • discreet • unflappable  
**Starting cash:** £350,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Preserve the legal record around Helix.**
2. **Keep Lee Verage’s lawful removal plan private until succession opens.**

### YOUR POWER — LEGAL REVIEW

**Once per game**, choose one currently available legal, governance or transaction document.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **legal / governance analysis** for that item.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are an M&A lawyer who has advised Vantage House Group on transactions, governance and difficult moments when everybody suddenly becomes very interested in exactly what a document says.

You are paid to distinguish:

- what happened;
- what somebody intended;
- what a document actually authorises;
- and what people later claim it meant.

You dislike sloppy language.

Tonight is full of it.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian was commercially brilliant and legally impatient.

He liked lawyers when they found routes forward.

He liked them less when they explained why a route was dangerous.

Recently, Julian asked you about the consequences of suspending the Helix transaction.

That told you something important:

he was considering a serious intervention.

You also helped Lee Verage explore a lawful route to remove or constrain Julian through board/governance action.

That work was not a murder plot.

Unfortunately, it may look like one.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Julian was seriously considering stopping or suspending Helix.
- He understood that doing so could trigger major legal, financial and governance consequences.
- Lee had already asked you to explore lawful ways to remove or constrain Julian.
- Lee’s plan involved board/governance action, not violence.
- Vantage’s paper trail matters because people are already rewriting what they “always knew.”
- Some of the most suspicious-looking documents may become less suspicious once their legal purpose is understood.

## HOW TO PLAY CLARA

Listen for people collapsing different ideas into one another.

For example:

> “Lee wanted Julian removed, therefore Lee wanted Julian dead.”

That is not the same statement.

Or:

> “This document changed, therefore the change was fraudulent.”

Maybe.

Maybe not.

Ask what the document actually proves.

Your value is not being the loudest person in the room.

It is being the person who notices when everybody else has skipped a logical step.

# Hugh Yield
## Founder, Apex Absolute Return Fund
### Played by: Yu

**Character vibe:** transactional, shrewd  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £1,200,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Hugh Yield.

You run a hedge fund.

Your basic philosophy is:

> Everything has a price.

You invested in Vantage early enough to become extremely rich if the Helix transaction completes.

You also know enough about Alexander to suspect that "extremely rich" and "absolutely doomed" may be separated by roughly one regulatory investigation.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Several days ago, somebody anonymously offered to sell you evidence suggesting Vantage's valuation was inflated.

You did not buy it.

The asking price was ridiculous.

You now regret this.

You still have the anonymous message.

It may help identify who already knew about the fraud.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You have personally taken a large financial position that depends on tomorrow's Helix transaction completing.

If the deal collapses, you lose an embarrassing amount of money.

If people discover the size of your position, you instantly acquire a very obvious motive.

You did not kill Alexander.

But financially, his death may benefit you.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Make profitable trades

Information is an asset.

Buy low.

Sell high.

Do not give things away because somebody asked nicely.

## 2. Discover who offered you the fraud evidence

They knew something before Alexander died.

Find them.

## 3. Protect your financial position

You desperately want to know whether Helix will still complete the transaction.

Use money and influence to shape the outcome.

## 4. Solve the murder

Good information is usually profitable information.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## DEEP POCKETS

You begin with one of the largest cash reserves in the game.

You may freely:

- buy evidence from players;
- fund alliances;
- pay for information;
- bribe people;
- finance eligible Host investigations.

You cannot buy official evidence directly unless the Host offers that service.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- someone tried to sell you evidence of Vantage fraud before the murder;
- Alexander's death may affect the Helix deal;
- your own financial exposure is enormous;
- several people in the room urgently need money.

---

# HOW TO PLAY HUGH

You do not need to discover everything yourself.

Somebody else can do the work.

Then you can buy it from them.

Try to finish the game richer in information, money or influence than you started.

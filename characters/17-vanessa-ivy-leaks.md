# Ivy Leaks
## Corporate Intelligence Consultant
### Played by: Vanessa

**Character vibe:** sly, observant  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £280,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Ivy Leaks.

Companies hire you when they want information they technically could have found themselves...

if they were willing to ask much more inappropriate questions.

You conduct:

- background investigations;
- discreet surveillance;
- competitor research;
- reputational due diligence;
- and things described on invoices as "strategic intelligence".

You have worked for more than one person at tonight's gala.

Not all of them know this.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Someone hired you to investigate Alexander Vane several weeks ago.

Your investigation uncovered:

- unusual financial activity;
- private meetings Alexander had not disclosed;
- and evidence that he had become suspicious of people inside Vantage.

Before you could finish your report, Alexander died.

Your client still expects results.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You accepted a second job involving another person at the gala.

The two clients have conflicting interests.

If either learns you were working both sides, your professional reputation becomes:

**complicated.**

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Discover who killed Alexander

The murder has made your unfinished investigation considerably more valuable.

## 2. Identify who knew about Vantage's problems before tonight

Someone was moving before Alexander died.

Find out who.

## 3. Protect your client list

People will want to know who hired you.

Do not give that information away cheaply.

## 4. Make money from intelligence

You possess information.

Other people possess money.

This seems solvable.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## BACKGROUND CHECK

Once during the game, choose one eligible character and privately ask the Host for a **Background Check**.

If a prepared file exists, you receive one additional verified fact about that character's corporate history.

It will never reveal whether they are "the murderer".

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was privately investigating people around him;
- some of Vantage's internal problems pre-date tonight;
- at least one person had begun preparing for Alexander to lose power;
- several guests are lying about their relationship with him.

---

# HOW TO PLAY IVY

Listen carefully.

People frequently reveal more when they believe they are talking to someone neutral.

You are not neutral.

# Lady Penny Sterling
## Trustee & Long-Term Vantage Shareholder
### Played by: Jane

**Character vibe:** eccentric, genteel  
**Role size:** Light  
**Starting cash:** £1,000,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Lady Penny Sterling.

Your family invested in Vantage when it was barely more than:

- a PowerPoint deck;
- several ambitious young men;
- and a suspiciously optimistic spreadsheet.

Your trust still owns a meaningful stake.

You have watched Vantage grow from startup to supposed £1bn institution.

You are not entirely convinced this was progress.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Your family trust owns an old class of Vantage shares with unusual voting rights.

Most newer investors either:

- do not understand them;
- have forgotten about them;
- or assume they no longer matter.

They matter.

Especially now that Alexander is dead.

---

# YOUR SECRET

Several years ago, your trust quietly sold part of its economic interest while retaining more voting influence than most people realise.

If the ownership structure becomes important tonight, you may have considerably more power than expected.

You have never felt a need to explain this.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Protect the family investment

Do not allow Vantage to be handed to an idiot.

## 2. Use your voting influence wisely

The Host will explain how your shares affect the succession process.

## 3. Discover whether the company is actually worth anything

You have heard increasingly strange rumours about the accounts.

## 4. Enjoy yourself

You are rich enough not to panic.

This is an advantage.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## LEGACY VOTE

When the formal succession process opens, you receive an additional voting entitlement.

You may:

- support a candidate;
- bargain for concessions;
- abstain;
- change your mind before the final ballot.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- your family's voting rights are unusually valuable;
- Alexander had recently asked lawyers about old shareholder agreements;
- some investors appear far more nervous about Helix than they admit.

---

# HOW TO PLAY PENNY

You do not need to chase every clue.

People may come to you because they want your vote.

Make them explain themselves.

You might learn quite a lot.

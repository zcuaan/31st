# Paige Turner
## Junior Research Analyst
### Played by: Rhiannon

**Character vibe:** curious, straightforward  
**Role size:** Light  
**Starting cash:** £70,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Paige Turner.

You are one of the more junior people at Vantage.

This means senior executives routinely:

- forget you are in the room;
- discuss confidential things beside you;
- ask you to print documents;
- and assume you understand less than you do.

This has been educational.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Earlier this week, Alexander asked you to research several companies connected to Vantage's reported assets.

One of them was strange.

The company:

- had almost no visible operations;
- shared an address with another Vantage-linked entity;
- and appeared repeatedly in internal documents.

Alexander told you:

> "Don't send this around yet."

You kept your notes.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You accidentally opened a confidential executive document you were not authorised to access.

You read enough to realise senior people were discussing what would happen if Alexander stopped the Helix deal.

You closed it.

You told nobody.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Find out why Alexander asked you to research those companies

Your research may connect to the financial scandal.

## 2. Solve the murder

You are here to investigate.

Follow clues and compare information.

## 3. Decide who to trust

You are not required to lie constantly.

But you also do not have to give your research to the first person who asks.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## RESEARCH REQUEST

Once during the game, you may bring one eligible company name or document reference to the Host.

If a prepared public-record search exists, you receive it.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was researching unusual Vantage-linked companies;
- at least one company looked suspicious;
- senior people had already discussed a future without Alexander;
- you have notes that may become valuable.

---

# HOW TO PLAY PAIGE

You have fewer secrets than some people.

That does not make you less useful.

Your advantage is that you can concentrate on the actual mystery while everybody else is desperately hiding crimes.

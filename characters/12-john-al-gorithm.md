## WHO YOU ARE

You are Al Gorithm.

You design the models and systems that allow Vantage executives to say things like:

> "The data speaks for itself."

Usually immediately before misunderstanding the data.

You think in:

- timestamps;
- patterns;
- anomalies;
- probabilities;
- and systems that should behave predictably but somehow do not.

Tonight is full of things that do not behave predictably.

Excellent.

Earlier this week, Alexander asked you to extract raw system data relating to:

- executive access;

- and certain valuation calculations.

# YOUR SECRET

Instead of reporting it immediately, you experimented with it.

But if Audit discovers what you did, you are going to have an extremely irritating meeting.

You do not need to become a master liar.

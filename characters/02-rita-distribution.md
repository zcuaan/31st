# RITA DISTRIBUTION
## Chief of Staff, Vantage House Group

**Vibe:** controlled • brilliant • political  
**Starting cash:** £150,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep your role in Julian’s death secret.**
2. **Protect Cole Control’s involvement without making yourself look obviously loyal to him.**
3. **Get the next CEO to make two concrete structural or governance commitments.**

### YOUR POWER — CHIEF OF STAFF ACCESS

**Once per game**, choose one **10-minute window before Julian’s death**.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **official diary / logistics record** for that window.

This is the official record, not omniscient truth.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Julian Vane’s Chief of Staff.

For years, you have been the person who turns Julian’s ideas, demands, meetings, crises and contradictions into something the rest of Vantage can actually survive.

You are openly communist.

You are also extremely well paid.

You see no contradiction in that.

Your view is simple: understanding how power works is not the same thing as approving of it, and proximity to power can sometimes be used to restrain it.

For a long time, you believed you could restrain Julian.

Eventually, you stopped believing that.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

You knew Julian better than almost anyone at Vantage.

You knew his intelligence, generosity, charm and extraordinary ability to make people feel personally chosen.

You also knew how much power he had accumulated around himself.

Julian could be genuinely helpful and genuinely ruthless in the same afternoon.

Recently, he had become alarmed by what he was seeing in Vantage’s financial reporting and the Helix transaction.

He intended to take serious action the next morning.

That action could have included stopping Helix, exposing irregularities and restructuring senior leadership.

You believed Julian would also make sure the public story left **Julian Vane looking like the person who finally saved Vantage from problems created under his own leadership**.

You found that intolerable.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

**Cole Control planned Julian’s murder.**

You knew.

You knowingly helped him.

Cole arranged for a **counterfeit version of Julian’s Neraval device** to exist.

During the security disruption, you used your legitimate Chief of Staff access to enter the relevant private area.

At approximately **16:58**, you:

- removed Julian’s genuine Neraval;
- replaced it with the counterfeit;
- later hid the genuine device inside the **Event Operations Case**;
- returned to the gala.

You knew the replacement was intended to kill Julian.

You believe the plan worked.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Cole planned for Julian to die.
- Cole arranged the counterfeit Neraval.
- You performed the physical swap.
- Cole told you there would be a short period of degraded security visibility.
- You do **not** know exactly how Cole learned that the weak-security window would happen.
- Julian intended serious action on Helix and senior leadership the following morning.
- Julian had been gathering evidence about reporting, approvals and governance.
- Cole believed Julian’s next move could destroy careers, jobs, value and the institution itself.
- You believed removing Julian might prevent one man from controlling the entire story of Vantage.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

Everything that matters.

You must hide:

- that you knew Cole intended to kill Julian;
- that you knowingly helped him;
- that you physically swapped the Neraval;
- that you hid the genuine device;
- the true nature of your coordination with Cole.

Your safest public story is built mostly from the truth:

You were working.

You moved around because you were Chief of Staff.

There was a security disruption.

You had legitimate access to event and executive areas.

You were trying to keep the gala functioning.

The dangerous part is the one thing you actually did during that window.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Cole Control — Chair of Audit & Risk
Your co-conspirator.

Cole believes institutions fail when too much power sits with one person.

He decided that removing Julian from office was no longer enough.

You agreed to help.

Do not behave like his bodyguard.

If you defend him too obviously, you may destroy both of you.

### Max Ledger — Group Strategy Director
Politically, you disagree with almost everything Max represents.

Professionally, you respect that he is competent and dangerous.

His financial decisions helped create the crisis Julian was preparing to expose.

### Alec Cation — People & Organisational Strategy
Alec cares about worker impact and structural power.

You may find some common ground with him, even if your methods and politics are not identical.

He may also be useful when the CEO candidates start making promises.

---

## HOW TO PLAY RITA

Be controlled.

You are not nervous because people are shouting.

You are nervous when somebody quietly produces the right document.

Do not over-defend Cole.

Do not rush to explain your movements unless somebody asks.

Use your real knowledge of Julian, Vantage and the gala to sound useful rather than evasive.

Push the succession conversation toward **structures**, not personalities.

Ask candidates what they will actually change:

- oversight
- worker influence
- board power
- founder power
- transparency
- independent controls

You helped kill Julian.

That does not mean you think every criticism of Julian was false.

It also does not mean you should confess because somebody agrees with you politically.

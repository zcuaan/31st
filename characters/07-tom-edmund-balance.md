# Edmund Balance
## Chair of Audit & Risk, Vantage House Capital
### Played by: Tom

**Character vibe:** gentle, quietly perceptive  
**Role size:** Medium-high  
**Starting cash:** £450,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** Yes, technically

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Edmund Balance.

You chair Vantage's Audit & Risk Committee.

You are calm, polite and generally considered one of the few adults in the building.

People tend to talk around you because you do not look threatening.

This has been extremely useful.

You have spent months becoming increasingly uncomfortable with Vantage's numbers.

Alexander recently started asking some of the same questions.

Then he died.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You have already noticed several inconsistencies in Vantage's internal reporting.

None of them individually proves fraud.

Together, they are worrying.

You also know Alexander requested an emergency private meeting with you for tomorrow morning.

The meeting subject was simply:

**"Helix / numbers / urgent."**

You never found out what he intended to tell you.

---

# YOUR SECRET

Months ago, you approved an internal control exception that should never have been approved.

At the time it looked harmless.

If Vantage's accounting scandal becomes public, people may ask why the Audit Chair allowed it.

You are not guilty of the central fraud.

But you are not completely clean either.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Work out what Alexander discovered

Follow the numbers.

Find documents.

Compare stories.

## 2. Protect yourself

Do not let your own control failure become the easiest person to blame for Vantage's collapse.

## 3. Influence the succession

You do not necessarily want to become CEO.

But you care deeply about who does.

Support someone you believe will protect the company — or your reputation.

## 4. Solve the murder

You are good at noticing when two apparently true things cannot both be true.

Use that.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## AUDIT REQUEST

Once during the game, you may ask the Host for an **Audit Review** of one eligible financial or corporate document you have found.

If a prepared review exists, you receive the corresponding analysis.

This can accelerate your investigation.

It will never be the only route to an essential conclusion.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander had concerns about Vantage's numbers;
- he requested an urgent meeting for tomorrow;
- some reported figures do not reconcile cleanly;
- multiple senior people benefit enormously if the Helix deal completes.

---

# HOW TO PLAY EDMUND

You do not need to shout.

Ask people simple questions.

Remember their answers.

Come back later.

Your advantage is that people are likely to underestimate how much you have noticed.

# VINCE TURE
## Crypto Founder / Venture Capitalist

**Vibe:** confident • opportunistic • deal-obsessed  
**Starting cash:** £850,000  
**CEO eligible:** YES

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep your commission to Opal Ration hidden until the CEO vote.**
2. **Monetise or use the Helix information you paid for to create at least £250,000 of value.**

### YOUR POWER — DEAL FLOW

**Once per game**, choose one currently available corporate or financial item.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **commercial-value / deal-context note**.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a crypto founder and venture capitalist.

You like information early, leverage cheap and opportunities before they become respectable.

You are used to people laughing at a deal right up until they ask whether there is still room in it.

Vantage interests you because:

- it has scale;
- it has institutional credibility;
- it has messy incentives;
- and somebody is almost always sitting on information worth more than they realise.

You recently paid Opal Ration to obtain confidential Helix material.

That decision is about to become awkward.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian respected ambition.

You respected people who built things large enough to matter.

You did not trust his instinct to control every important narrative.

You were interested in Helix because the transaction could create enormous commercial advantage for whoever understood it first.

You did not want Julian dead.

You wanted information he did not want you to have.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

You commissioned **Opal Ration** to obtain confidential Helix information.

You paid for access to material you were not entitled to receive.

You knew Opal would need to do something improper to get it.

You did **not** know Opal would cause a security blackout.

You did **not** know anybody would use that window for murder.

You now have a problem:

the information you wanted may be valuable,

but the method used to obtain it may connect you to the most suspicious part of the night.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- You paid Opal for confidential Helix information.
- Opal successfully obtained targeted material.
- The information may be commercially valuable.
- You do not know exactly how Opal obtained it.
- You do not know who else may have noticed or exploited the same security weakness.
- Cass Flow may be a buyer, rival or useful market interpreter.
- Arby Trage may value distressed or asymmetric opportunities.
- If you run for CEO, your information-theft scandal could become a major liability.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### The commission

You asked Opal to steal confidential information.

That is the core secret.

### The information

You want to monetise or use it before everybody understands where it came from.

You can:

- sell it;
- trade access;
- use it to negotiate;
- use it to support a CEO campaign;
- exchange it for another advantage.

You cannot invent what the material says.

---

## BECOMING CEO

You are eligible to run for Interim CEO.

To get onto the ballot, you need:

- a **£200,000 non-refundable nomination deposit**;
- signatures from **two voting shareholders**.

To win, you need **5 of the 9 weighted votes**.

You do not need to run.

But a corporate crisis is exactly the kind of moment when outsiders suddenly become plausible.

You may disagree about what the information is now worth.

### Cass Flow — Hedge Fund Investor
Cass understands how information becomes money.

That makes Cass a buyer, rival or both.

### Arby Trage — Special Situations Investor / Restructuring Adviser
Arby likes unusual opportunities and distressed situations.

---

## HOW TO PLAY VINCE

Think commercially.

Ask:

- who needs this information;
- who is afraid of it;
- what is it worth before it becomes public;
- can it buy votes, money or protection?

Your secret is serious.

But do not behave as if the blackout automatically makes you a murderer.

Your crime was information theft.

Find out what else happened during that window.

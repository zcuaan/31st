# Barry Leverage
## Private Equity Partner & Vantage Board Director
### Played by: Jack

**Character vibe:** ambitious, competitive  
**Role size:** Medium-high  
**Starting cash:** £900,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** Yes

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Barry Leverage.

You work in private equity.

You describe yourself as a "long-term value creator".

Other people use different words.

Your fund invested heavily in Vantage and you have spent years tolerating Alexander Vane because he made everyone rich.

Now Alexander is dead.

And Vantage needs leadership.

Convenient.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You had already been preparing for the possibility that Alexander might lose control of the company.

You have a confidential draft restructuring plan that would:

- remove several senior executives;
- reduce founder control;
- and give your fund significantly more influence.

Alexander discovered the plan recently.

Your final conversation with him was extremely unpleasant.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You have been quietly sounding out shareholders about replacing Alexander.

You did NOT kill him.

But if messages showing your coup attempt become public, you will look extremely suspicious.

Especially because Alexander died immediately before you could launch it.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Gain control

You may:

- run for CEO;
- back another candidate;
- engineer a takeover;
- negotiate with Oliver.

Whatever produces the most leverage.

## 2. Keep your coup plan from looking like a murder motive

People can know you wanted Alexander gone from the company.

Preferably they do not conclude you wanted him gone from existence.

## 3. Make money

Trade aggressively.

Buy information.

Acquire votes.

Exploit distress.

## 4. Solve the murder

A murderer inside Vantage is a material investment risk.

Very inconvenient.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## HOSTILE BID

Once the succession process opens, you may spend Vantage Money to formally launch a leadership challenge or financially support another candidate.

The Host will explain the exact cost when the board process begins.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was considering disrupting the Helix deal;
- several investors would lose huge amounts if he did;
- succession planning had already begun quietly;
- Alexander had enemies on the board.

---

# HOW TO PLAY BARRY

Everything is negotiable.

Information.

Votes.

Support.

Loyalty.

Especially loyalty.

# AUDIE TRAIL
## Internal Audit

**Vibe:** methodical • stubborn • quietly dangerous  
**Starting cash:** £100,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Establish that Vantage’s reporting did not match the underlying source material.**
2. **Get a serious CEO candidate to commit to independent audit and whistleblower protection.**

### YOUR POWER — SOURCE CHECK

**Once per game**, choose one currently available numeric, financial or corporate document.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **source reconciliation**.

This tells you whether the document matches the underlying source record and, if not, what kind of mismatch exists.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You work in Internal Audit.

You are the person people remember exists when something has already gone wrong.

Your job is not to have an opinion about whether a number feels reasonable.

Your job is to ask:

> **Where did it come from?**

You quietly copied source material because you became concerned that some Vantage reporting no longer matched the records beneath it.

You did not know Julian would be dead by the end of the gala.

Now those copies may matter far more than you expected.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian liked results.

Audit liked provenance.

This created tension.

He could be surprisingly supportive when he believed a control genuinely protected the business.

He could also become intensely impatient when audit threatened speed, valuation or a transaction he wanted.

Recently, you became concerned that the gap between Vantage’s headline story and the source records was becoming too large to dismiss as presentation.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

You copied audit/source material without waiting for the normal internal process.

You did it because you were worried records could change, disappear or be reframed before anyone outside the immediate chain understood the problem.

That decision may be defensible.

It was still not normal procedure.

You need the evidence to survive.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Some reported numbers / claims do not match underlying source material.
- The mismatch is not just a formatting issue.
- Max Ledger pushed aggressive interpretations and control exceptions.
- Vera Iance raised concerns about assumptions.
- Reese Search may be able to corroborate some issues using public records.
- Julian had started collecting old approvals and internal data before his death.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

You preserved copied material outside the normal audit workflow.

If the wrong person frames that as theft or misconduct, you may lose control of the story.

Your best defence is to prove the material is genuine and useful.

You do not want the evidence buried.

You also do not want to become the person blamed for leaking it carelessly.

---

You and Alec are old friends.

You trust Alec more than most people in the room.

### Reese Search — Junior Research Analyst
Reese may be able to connect public information to the internal mismatches you found.

### Max Ledger — Group Strategy Director
Max’s decisions sit close to the audit trail you are worried about.

---

## HOW TO PLAY AUDIE

Do not argue from vibes.

Ask for the source.

Ask who approved the change.

Ask whether a number is:

- copied;
- adjusted;
- forecast;
- reclassified;
- or simply wrong.

Your murder role is indirect.

The financial scandal creates motive.

It does not automatically identify a killer.

You are one of the people who can help the room keep those ideas separate.

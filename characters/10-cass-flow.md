## WHO YOU ARE

You are a hedge fund investor.

You are not emotionally invested in Vantage being good, bad, heroic or corrupt.

You are invested in being **right before everybody else**.

A corporate murder is horrifying.

It is also information.

A succession crisis is destabilising.

It is also information.

A hidden financial problem is dangerous.

It is also information.

You came to the gala with money.

You intend to leave with more.

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian respected investors who could move quickly and hated investors who could move against him.

You admired his ability to create value.

You never confused that with trust.

You were interested in Vantage because the company looked strong enough to matter and unstable enough to misprice.

Julian’s death has made that question much more urgent.

## HOW TO PLAY CASS

Treat information like an asset.

Ask:

- Is it true?
- Is it public?
- Who else knows?
- Who wants it?
- Does it change the CEO race?
- Does it change what Vantage is worth?

Do not give away useful information for free unless doing so improves your position.

Your murder-investigation incentive is straightforward:

> if you understand what really happened before everyone else, you can trade that understanding.

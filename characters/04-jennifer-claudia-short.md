# Claudia Short
## Activist Short-Seller & Financial Investigator
### Played by: Jennifer

**Character vibe:** sharp, mischievous  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £200,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Claudia Short.

You make money when companies fail.

Vantage House Capital has always looked too perfect to you.

The £1bn valuation.

The miraculous growth.

The conveniently flattering numbers.

You have spent months quietly investigating Alexander Vane and Vantage.

And you found something.

Unfortunately, Alexander found out that YOU found something.

---

# PRIVATE — THE TRUTH

Before Alexander's body was officially discovered, you secretly entered his private office.

You were looking for a USB drive.

That USB contained information which could expose both Vantage's financial problems and the illegal methods you used to obtain some of your evidence.

When you entered the office, Alexander was collapsed and unresponsive.

You believed he was already dead.

You panicked.

You took the USB.

While doing so, you disturbed the scene and moved several objects.

Then you left.

You told nobody.

---

# THE TERRIFYING PART

There is evidence you entered Alexander's office before his body was officially found.

If that comes out, you look unbelievably guilty.

You:

- lied about your movements;
- secretly entered the office;
- encountered Alexander's body;
- stole evidence;
- disturbed the scene;
- and left without telling anyone.

That is an awful murder defence.

---

# WHAT YOU DO NOT KNOW

Alexander was NOT necessarily dead when you found him.

You did not check properly.

You were focused on the USB and getting out.

One of the biggest questions in the entire investigation is:

**Was Alexander still alive when you left?**

You need that answer more than almost anyone.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Prove you did not kill Alexander

You may have committed several other crimes.

Murder was not one of them.

## 2. Keep possession of the USB — or control what happens to it

The USB contains extremely valuable and extremely dangerous information.

## 3. Expose Vantage's fraud

You have money and reputation riding on Vantage collapsing.

You want the truth out.

## 4. Reconstruct the real death timeline

If you can prove Alexander remained alive after you left, your entire situation changes.

---

# YOUR ADVANTAGES

### Financial Investigation
You understand suspicious transactions and corporate structures.

### The USB
You begin with something other players may desperately want.

### Incentive to Investigate
You have one of the strongest reasons in the room to work out the exact time of death.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was collapsed when you entered.
- he appeared unresponsive.
- the USB was connected to or near his laptop.
- you removed it.
- you disturbed the scene.
- you left before the official discovery.

You do NOT know what actually caused Alexander's collapse or death.

---

# HOW TO PLAY CLAUDIA

You can lie about whether you entered the office.

You can lie about the USB.

You can lie about what you were investigating.

But sooner or later evidence may point towards you.

Your best defence may eventually become the truth:

> "Yes, I found him. Yes, I stole something. No, I did not kill him."

The problem is making anybody believe you.

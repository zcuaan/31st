# Audie Trail
## Internal Audit Manager, Vantage House Capital
### Played by: Chris

**Character vibe:** earnest, analytical  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £90,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Audie Trail.

You work in Internal Audit.

This means everybody is friendly until you ask them for supporting documentation.

You have spent months reviewing Vantage's internal controls.

There are problems.

Lots of problems.

Most are boring.

A few are not.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You recently discovered that several financial records connected to Vantage's valuation do not match their source data.

You began tracing the discrepancies.

The trail appeared to involve people significantly more senior than you.

So you made copies.

You had planned to speak to Alexander privately after the gala.

Now Alexander is dead.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You have confidential audit material you were not authorised to remove from Vantage's systems.

You copied it because you were afraid the records might disappear.

Technically, this is misconduct.

Practically, it may now be some of the most important evidence in the building.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Work out who manipulated the records

Follow the audit trail.

Do not assume the first suspicious person is responsible for everything.

## 2. Protect your evidence

You need the documents.

Other people may want them destroyed.

## 3. Stay out of the political circus

You are not trying to become CEO.

You are trying to establish what happened.

## 4. Solve Alexander's murder

You believe the financial irregularities and the murder may be connected.

Find out whether you are right.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## SOURCE CHECK

Once during the game, you may bring the Host one eligible financial document and request:

**SOURCE CHECK**

If a source comparison exists, you receive it.

This can reveal whether a figure matches the underlying record.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- at least some Vantage financial reporting is inconsistent with source data;
- Alexander was becoming concerned;
- you intended to meet him after the gala;
- the discrepancies involve senior-level access or approval.

---

# HOW TO PLAY AUDIE

You are not supposed to be a brilliant liar.

That is fine.

Your strength is having facts that other people do not.

Ask questions.

Compare documents.

Notice when explanations do not match the records.

And if somebody offers you £300,000 to lose an audit file...

you have a decision to make.

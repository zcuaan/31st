## WHO YOU ARE

You are Grant Interest.

You are a private banker and non-executive director of Vantage.

You spend much of your professional life knowing:

- who has money;
- who owes money;
- who says they have money;
- and which of those are different things.

This evening contains several excellent examples.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You know that several people connected to Vantage have borrowed heavily against assets whose value depends on the Helix transaction.

Some of those loans become uncomfortable if the deal fails.

One borrower in particular is far more exposed than anyone publicly realises.

You are bound by confidentiality.

Mostly.

---

# YOUR SECRET

Your bank extended one loan on terms that would be extremely embarrassing if scrutinised.

You pushed the deal through because the client was important.

If regulators start looking at Vantage-linked lending, you may have your own problem.

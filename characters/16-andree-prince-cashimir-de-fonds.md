# Prince Cashimir de Fonds
## Heir to the de Fonds Family Office
### Played by: Andree

**Character vibe:** grandiose, scheming  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £700,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Prince Cashimir de Fonds.

Your family has:

- a title;
- several extremely impressive houses;
- portraits of dead relatives everywhere;
- and significantly less liquid cash than people assume.

The de Fonds Family Office invested heavily in Vantage House Capital.

Publicly, you describe this as:

> "a long-term strategic allocation."

Privately, your family desperately needs the Helix transaction to complete.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Your family's Vantage shares have been pledged as security against several enormous loans.

If the Helix deal completes, everything is manageable.

If it collapses, lenders may start asking extremely impolite questions.

Alexander knew this.

A few days ago, he joked:

> "Careful, Cashimir. One bad announcement and you'll have to sell a château."

You did not find this funny.

---

# YOUR SECRET

Your family is nowhere near as wealthy as everyone believes.

Much of your apparent fortune is:

- property;
- debt;
- inherited assets;
- and creative presentation.

You urgently need liquidity.

The room must not realise just how urgently.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Keep the Helix deal alive

The transaction matters to you enormously.

Find out whether it can still proceed.

## 2. Raise cash

Trade.

Make deals.

Sell information.

Do not end the evening poor.

## 3. Protect the family reputation

Nobody needs to know about the loans.

Especially the bankers.

## 4. Solve Alexander's murder

If someone killed Alexander because of the Helix deal, that may affect your family's future too.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## FAMILY CONNECTIONS

Once during the game, you may ask the Host for one eligible:

- ownership record;
- historic investor record;
- or family-office connection

linked to a company or person you are investigating.

If a prepared record exists, you receive it.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was becoming unpredictable about Helix;
- several investors are financially dependent on the transaction;
- your own family is more exposed than almost anyone realises;
- Alexander enjoyed knowing people's weaknesses.

---

# HOW TO PLAY CASHIMIR

Behave as though money is beneath you.

Then aggressively acquire as much of it as possible.

Your title is real.

Your liquidity is not.

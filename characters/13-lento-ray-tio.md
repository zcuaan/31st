# Ray Tio
## Director of Risk Modelling
### Played by: Lento

**Character vibe:** analytical, reserved  
**Role size:** Medium  
**Starting cash:** £180,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Ray Tio.

You run risk models for people who routinely ignore them.

Your job is to calculate exactly how catastrophic an event might be.

Vantage executives then describe your worst-case scenario as:

> "a little pessimistic."

Alexander's death has made your models considerably more interesting.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You have been analysing the Helix transaction.

According to the official numbers, it looks excellent.

According to several underlying datasets, it makes considerably less sense.

Some metrics appear to have been:

- counted twice;
- shifted between reporting periods;
- or presented using assumptions that are extremely generous.

You cannot yet prove deliberate fraud.

But somebody has been massaging the numbers.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You privately warned Alexander months ago that some of Vantage's valuation assumptions were indefensible.

He told you to stop putting concerns in writing.

You did.

That decision now looks terrible.

If the scandal erupts, you may be accused of knowing about it and staying silent.

Which is, unfortunately, partly true.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Prove what happened to Vantage's numbers

Find enough evidence to distinguish:

**aggressive accounting**

from

**deliberate fraud**.

## 2. Protect yourself

You raised concerns.

Then you stopped raising them.

Make sure people understand the first part.

## 3. Investigate Alexander's death

Financial motives may be more important than personal grudges.

## 4. Trade analysis carefully

Raw evidence may be valuable.

Your interpretation of it may be even more valuable.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## RISK MODEL

Once during the game, you may bring the Host one eligible set of financial figures.

If a prepared model exists, you receive a short analysis identifying the most important inconsistency.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Vantage's headline valuation does not cleanly match the underlying numbers;
- Alexander knew you had concerns;
- Helix is relying on those figures;
- several people stand to lose enormous amounts if the deal collapses.

---

# HOW TO PLAY RAY

You do not need to tell everyone everything immediately.

If you work something out, decide:

- who needs to know;
- who might pay;
- and who absolutely should not hear it yet.

Numbers do not lie.

People interpreting them absolutely do.

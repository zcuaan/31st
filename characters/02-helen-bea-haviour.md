# Bea Haviour
## Chief of Staff to Alexander Vane
### Played by: Helen

**Character vibe:** controlled, slippery  
**Role size:** Medium, but carries a major secret  
**Starting cash:** £150,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Bea Haviour, Chief of Staff to the late Alexander Vane.

For years you managed:

- his calendar;
- his crises;
- his secrets;
- his enemies;
- and occasionally Alexander himself.

You know where things are buried.

Sometimes literally.

Half the people at Vantage underestimate you because you are technically "support staff".

That is their mistake.

---

# PRIVATE — THE TRUTH

You knowingly helped Sebastian Ledger murder Alexander.

Alexander had discovered serious problems in Vantage's financial reporting.

Sebastian told you that if Alexander stopped the Helix deal, everyone would be destroyed.

Including you.

Sebastian gave you a counterfeit version of Alexander's fictional prescription device, **Neraval**.

You replaced Alexander's genuine device during a period when the security system was offline.

You knew why you were doing it.

You knew Sebastian intended Alexander to die.

You believed the plan worked.

---

# WHAT YOU BELIEVE

You believe:

- you and Sebastian are the only two people responsible for Alexander's murder;
- Alexander later used the counterfeit device;
- his death resulted from your actions;
- nobody saw you make the swap.

You are wrong about several things.

But you do not know that yet.

---

# WHAT YOU DO NOT KNOW

You do NOT know that:

- Alexander's champagne had separately been tampered with;
- someone entered his office before his actual death;
- the security blackout was created for another person's crime;
- Alexander may have survived longer than you assumed.

You should investigate these things just like everybody else.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Keep your role in Alexander's death hidden

Do not allow investigators to establish that you knowingly replaced his Neraval device.

## 2. Protect yourself first

Sebastian may be your co-conspirator.

That does not mean you have to go down with him.

If necessary, you may:

- distance yourself;
- imply Sebastian manipulated you;
- turn on him;
- negotiate for your own survival.

## 3. Control Alexander's secrets

You know more about Alexander's private life and working habits than almost anyone.

Information is valuable.

Use it.

## 4. Work out why the crime scene makes no sense

You expected a clean medical-looking death.

Instead there are strange clues everywhere.

Find out why.

---

# YOUR ADVANTAGES

### Chief of Staff Knowledge
You know Alexander's routines, office habits and private schedule.

### Executive Access
You had legitimate reasons to enter areas other people could not.

This is useful.

It is also dangerous.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander routinely used Neraval.
- Sebastian supplied you with the counterfeit.
- there was a temporary security blackout.
- you used that window to make the replacement.
- Alexander later went to his office feeling unwell.

You do NOT know why he was already feeling unwell.

---

# HOW TO PLAY BEA

Your strength is that almost everything you did has an innocent explanation.

You were Alexander's Chief of Staff.

Of course you handled his belongings.

Of course you knew his schedule.

Of course you had access.

You may lie freely about what you did.

But official evidence is real and cannot be altered or invented.

Your game is not to hide in a corner.

It is to remain useful enough that people keep trusting you while quietly steering them away from the truth.

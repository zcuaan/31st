# PERRY SPIN
## Crisis Communications & Westminster Affairs

**Vibe:** fast • charming • shameless  
**Starting cash:** £200,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Get retained by the incoming CEO.**
2. **Use the crisis to prove that controlling the story is valuable.**

### YOUR POWER — SPIN

**Once per game**, choose one piece of **registered/public evidence**.

Write a public statement of **30 words or fewer** about it.

Give it to the GM.

The GM will publish it as clearly labelled **commentary / spin**, not evidence.

You may mislead, reframe or distract.

You may not alter the underlying evidence.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You run crisis communications and Westminster affairs.

You understand that facts matter.

You also understand that facts rarely arrive without framing.

Your job is to decide which sentence gets quoted, which detail gets buried and which version of events sounds most believable before breakfast.

You are not embarrassed by that.

You think every institution does it.

You are simply better at it.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian understood narrative power instinctively.

He liked big messages, controlled access and the appearance of inevitability.

You often helped turn messy reality into something investors, politicians and journalists could live with.

You also knew Julian sometimes believed that if the story was persuasive enough, the underlying problem could wait.

Now he is dead.

The competition to control the story has become even more valuable.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Vantage is entering a reputational crisis as well as a murder investigation.
- CEO candidates will need help sounding stable, credible and decisive.
- Polly Tician understands political access and influence.
- Max Ledger may need somebody to separate his commercial wrongdoing from murder suspicion.
- Lee Verage may need help making ruthless governance sound responsible.
- At approximately **17:32**, you went looking for Julian when he missed his scheduled appearance.
- Because access was restricted, you got **Rita Distribution**.
- At approximately **17:34**, you and Rita entered and discovered Julian dead.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

Nothing murder-critical.

Your risk is professional:

You have built a career making difficult stories survivable.

Some people will see that as useful.

Others will assume that if a story was manipulated, you manipulated it.

You are comfortable operating in that ambiguity.

---

Polly understands influence, access and political risk.

### Max Ledger — Group Strategy Director
Max is a plausible CEO candidate with an obvious reputational problem.

That makes him a potential client.

### Lee Verage — Private Equity Partner / Board Director
Lee may need help turning cold boardroom logic into something people can actually support.

Another potential client.

---

## HOW TO PLAY PERRY

Talk to everyone.

Find out:

- what people are afraid will become public;
- which CEO candidate needs you most;
- what story the room currently believes;
- what registered evidence can be framed differently.

Your power does not create fake evidence.

It creates a **public interpretation**.

Use it when the room is already arguing about what a fact means.

Your best outcome is simple:

> by the end of the night, the new CEO thinks Vantage cannot survive without you.

## DO NOT INVENT

Do not invent new evidence, crimes, alibis, secret relationships, extra money, votes, powers, journalists or facts about Julian’s death.

You may speculate or lie about established facts.

You may not create new canon.

---

## YOU MAY

You may lie, bluff, accuse, bribe, betray, trade information, make or break deals, publish spin through your power and steal unattended eligible game items.

# LUCY LEAKS
## Corporate Intelligence Adviser

**Vibe:** discreet • observant • transactional  
**Starting cash:** £300,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Sell useful intelligence to at least two different buyers for £300,000+ total.**
2. **Keep the fact that both Lee Verage and Hugh Stake hired you private until the CEO vote.**

### YOUR POWER — BACKGROUND CHECK

**Once per game**, choose one player character.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive one prepared **non-murder-critical intelligence note** about that character.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play and will not directly identify Julian’s killer.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a corporate intelligence adviser.

You are hired when somebody wants to know:

- who really controls a decision;
- what a rival is worried about;
- which relationship matters;
- what public story does not match the private one;
- and which question is worth asking next.

You are not a detective.

You are not a journalist.

You are a professional collector of useful context.

Tonight, useful context is suddenly worth a great deal of money.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian Vane was once a serious romantic partner.

The relationship ended.

You remained in overlapping professional and social circles.

You knew his charm, his ambition and his habit of treating information as leverage.

You also knew that being close to Julian could make people feel important right up until they realised he had learned more about them than they had learned about him.

You are not at the gala because of the old relationship.

But it means you understand Julian in a way many people do not.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

You are working for **two different clients** on succession intelligence.

### Hugh Stake
Hugh hired you to map:

- succession factions;
- candidate vulnerabilities;
- useful pressure points.

### Lee Verage
Lee separately hired you to understand:

- the voting landscape;
- rival candidates;
- which relationships could influence the succession.

Neither client knows about the other.

You would like to keep it that way until the vote is over.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Hugh Stake has enormous voting power.
- Lee Verage is serious about controlling Vantage’s future.
- The succession race will make private information valuable.
- Julian’s death will make people talk more freely than they normally would.
- People often reveal more when they think you already know something.
- Your old relationship with Julian may make some people trust you and others suspect you.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### Your two clients

If Hugh and Lee discover you are working for both of them, your value may collapse.

Or they may decide you are even more useful.

You would prefer to control when that happens.

### Julian

You and Julian had a serious past relationship.

You do not need to hide that forever.

But you do not want every question about your intelligence work reframed as jealousy, grief or revenge.

You are here to trade information, not perform your relationship history for the room.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Hugh Stake — Cornerstone Investor
Client number one.

He wants succession intelligence.

He controls three indivisible votes.

### Lee Verage — Private Equity Partner / Board Director
Client number two.

He is a CEO candidate and does not know Hugh also hired you.

### Polly Tician — Government Affairs / Lobbying
Polly understands influence and may need discreet information quickly.

She is also exactly the kind of person who knows information can become dangerous once it spreads.

---

## HOW TO PLAY LUCY

Listen more than you speak.

Ask specific questions.

Do not sell the same piece of information for the same reason if you can sell it twice for different reasons.

Your most useful sentence may be:

> **“What is that worth to you?”**

You still need to investigate Julian’s murder like everyone else.

But you do not have to care for moral reasons.

Murder information is valuable because:

- it changes candidate risk;
- it changes who can be trusted;
- it changes what people will pay to know first.

---

## MAKE LUCY YOURS

You may invent harmless flavour such as accent, clothing, mannerisms, hobbies, neighbourhood, catchphrases, intelligence habits, harmless personal history and props.

Possible energy:

**private intelligence consultant • elegant gossip broker • asks one question too many • remembers everything**

---

## DO NOT INVENT

Do not invent new evidence, clients, crimes, alibis, secret relationships, extra money, votes, powers or facts about Julian’s death.

You may speculate or lie about established facts.

You may not create new canon.

---

## YOU MAY

You may lie, bluff, accuse, bribe, betray, sell information, make or break deals, steal unattended eligible game items and hide portable evidence you legitimately obtain.

# Polly Tix
## Celebrity Lobbyist & Government Affairs Consultant
### Played by: Sara

**Character vibe:** dramatic, chaotic  
**Role size:** Medium-high  
**Starting cash:** £250,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Polly Tix.

Lobbyist.

Political operator.

Professional person-who-knows-someone.

You have:

- MPs on speed dial;
- journalists who owe you favours;
- clients you cannot publicly admit are clients;
- and an extremely flexible relationship with confidentiality.

Alexander Vane was one of your most difficult clients.

You hated him.

This is unfortunate given that he is now dead.

---

# PRIVATE — THE TRUTH

You tried to kill Alexander.

Alone.

You did NOT work with anyone else.

Before his death, you secretly put the fictional substance **Aster-9** into Alexander's champagne.

You believed the amount would kill him.

Shortly afterwards:

- he became confused;
- he appeared increasingly unwell;
- he disappeared from the gala;
- and later he was found dead.

So obviously:

**you think you killed him.**

You have told nobody.

---

# THE PROBLEM

Something is wrong.

The evidence does not quite behave the way you expected.

You may gradually discover that Aster-9 explains Alexander becoming unwell…

…but may NOT explain his death.

If that is true:

**who actually killed him?**

And why did your attempted murder happen at exactly the same time?

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Do not get exposed for attempted murder

Even if Aster-9 did not ultimately kill Alexander, you still deliberately tried to kill him.

That is not an especially comforting defence.

## 2. Find out whether you actually killed him

This matters to you enormously.

Follow the toxicology.

Follow the timeline.

Find out what happened after Alexander left the main room.

## 3. Survive the political fallout

You have clients, contacts and scandals everywhere.

Protect your reputation and your leverage.

## 4. Cause problems

You are allowed to be extremely involved in everybody else's business.

In fact, it would be strange if you weren't.

---

# YOUR ADVANTAGES

### Political Contacts
You know rumours and reputational information that other players may value.

### Social Access
You have a plausible reason to talk to basically everyone.

### Confidence
You can tell the truth, lie completely, or talk so much that nobody remembers what they originally asked you.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- you tampered with Alexander's champagne;
- Alexander drank from it;
- he became unwell afterwards;
- you believed Aster-9 would kill him;
- Alexander later died.

You do NOT know:

- that someone tampered with his Neraval device;
- that a separate murder plot existed;
- that someone entered his office while he was still alive.

---

# HOW TO PLAY POLLY

You are NOT part of a murder team.

Do not look for your "co-conspirators".

As far as you know there are none.

You should genuinely investigate the murder because the answer matters to you personally.

If evidence proves Aster-9 was not fatal, you have just learned something enormous:

**you tried to murder Alexander, but somebody else succeeded.**

Try not to scream.

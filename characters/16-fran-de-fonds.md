# FRAN DE FONDS
## Family Office / Shareholder

**Vibe:** polished • proud • financially cornered  
**Starting cash:** £700,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO  
**Succession votes:** 1

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Secure financing, relief or restructuring worth at least £500,000.**
2. **Keep the true depth of your financial distress private until the CEO vote.**

### YOUR POWER — FAMILY OFFICE ACCESS

**Once per game**, choose one currently available financial exposure directly involving you or your family office.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **private exposure / ownership context note**.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You represent a wealthy family office with old relationships, expensive habits and a reputation for being more financially secure than you currently feel.

From the outside, you look liquid.

You are not.

Your family office has become overextended.

A series of decisions that looked manageable separately now look dangerous together.

You need relief.

You would prefer nobody realise how badly.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian understood wealthy families very well.

He knew that “rich” and “liquid” are not the same thing.

He also knew that people will do surprising things to avoid admitting financial weakness in public.

You did business around Vantage because it offered access, credibility and financing.

You did not want Julian dead.

You did want the pressure around your finances to stop getting worse.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

Your family office is under much more financial pressure than most people in the room know.

**Cal Lateral** helped arrange or approve lending that kept you afloat.

Some of that lending may look questionable once the full exposure is understood.

You need:

- refinancing;
- relief;
- restructuring;
- new money;
- or a deal strong enough to buy you time.

Your vote is an asset.

So is your discretion.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Your financial position is precarious.
- Cal knows more about it than almost anyone else.
- Arby Trage may be interested in distressed or awkward situations.
- Penny Sterling knows you through an old family/social network connection.
- Your one succession vote may be worth considerably more than one ninth of the room’s attention.
- A candidate who needs your vote may be willing to help with your financial problem.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### The depth of the problem

You are not simply “a bit stretched.”

You need a serious solution.

If the room learns that before the CEO vote, your leverage may collapse.

People may start treating you as desperate rather than important.

### Cal’s involvement

Cal’s lending helped keep your position stable.

You do not want that relationship examined too closely unless you already have another route to safety.

---

## YOUR VOTE

You control **1 succession vote**.

You may:

- accept bribes;
- make promises;
- sign nomination papers;
- change your mind;
- betray a deal.

Your vote is secret.

A Deal Slip proves a promise existed.

It does not bind your ballot.

Cal knows the uncomfortable details of your borrowing and exposure.

### Arby Trage — Special Situations Investor / Restructuring Adviser

That makes Arby dangerous.

### Penny Sterling — Legacy Shareholder
You know Penny through an old family/social network connection.

It is not a major secret or subplot.

---

## HOW TO PLAY FRAN

Look solvent.

Negotiate before you become desperate.

Use your vote as leverage.

Your financial problem is not part of Julian’s murder.

But the crisis has changed everybody’s bargaining power.

You want to leave tonight with a real solution, not another promise that somebody will “look at it next week.”

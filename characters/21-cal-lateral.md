# CAL LATERAL
## Private Banker / Non-Executive Director

**Vibe:** smooth • cautious • commercially flexible  
**Starting cash:** £600,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO  
**Succession votes:** 1

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep your questionable lending to Fran de Fonds from becoming public before the CEO vote.**
2. **Secure repayment, relief or another concrete concession worth at least £200,000.**

### YOUR POWER — CREDIT CHECK

**Once per game**, choose one currently available borrower, financing arrangement or exposure.

Give your ability card to the GM to receive the prepared **credit / collateral context note**.

You may keep, trade, sell or reveal the result.

Your power cannot unlock evidence that is not yet in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are a private banker and Vantage non-executive director.

You understand a basic truth about wealthy people:

> money and liquidity are not the same thing.

Your job is to know who can actually pay, who merely looks like they can, and what somebody is willing to pledge when appearances start to crack.

You have recently been helping Fran de Fonds’ family office stay afloat.

The decisions involved are more aggressive than you would like examined in public.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian valued people who could make financing appear when conventional answers were too slow.

You understood that instinct.

You also understood the risk.

You did not want Julian dead.

You did want Vantage to remain stable enough that awkward lending decisions stayed manageable rather than becoming scandalous.

His death has made that much harder.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

You helped arrange or approve lending connected to **Fran de Fonds’ family office**.

Fran’s finances are much more precarious than most people know.

Your lending helped keep the situation going.

Some of the decisions may look:

- overly generous;
- badly secured;
- conflicted;
- or designed to postpone recognition of the real problem.

You need a solution before everyone starts asking why the exposure was allowed to grow.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Fran is under serious financial pressure.
- You know the exposure better than almost anyone.
- Arby Trage may provide restructuring or rescue capital.
- Hugh Stake has enough voting power to influence the next leadership team.
- Your one succession vote may be useful in negotiating protection or a solution.
- If Fran collapses publicly, your own judgement will come under scrutiny.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### The lending

You do not want the room examining the structure before you have a plan.

### Your own exposure

Fran’s problem is not only Fran’s problem.

If the lending is criticised or impaired, your judgement and position may suffer too.

You therefore need one of:

- repayment;
- refinancing;
- restructuring;
- protection;
- or a credible commitment that reduces your risk.

---

## YOUR VOTE

You control **1 succession vote**.

You may:

- accept bribes;
- sign nomination forms;
- trade support;
- demand concessions;
- break promises;
- vote secretly.

A Deal Slip proves a promise existed.

It does not bind your ballot.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Fran de Fonds — Family Office / Shareholder
Fran needs help.

You know how much.

That gives you leverage.

It also creates risk.

### Arby Trage — Special Situations Investor / Restructuring Adviser
Arby may be exactly the person who can solve Fran’s problem.

Arby may also make the solution expensive.

### Hugh Stake — Cornerstone Investor
Hugh controls three votes and may care deeply about the stability of Vantage’s shareholder base.

That can be useful.

---

## HOW TO PLAY CAL

Do not panic.

Financial distress is a negotiation until somebody calls it a collapse.

Keep Fran calm.

Work out what Arby wants.

Use your vote.

Try to leave the night with a real solution rather than a promise to “review the position.”

Your problem is serious.

It is not part of Julian’s murder.

# Lord Oliver Blackwood
## Cornerstone Investor & Shadow Chairman of Vantage House Capital
### Played by: Oli

**Character vibe:** powerful, enigmatic  
**Role size:** High  
**Starting cash:** £1,500,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Lord Oliver Blackwood.

You do not run companies.

You decide who gets to.

Your investment vehicle owns enough of Vantage House Capital — directly and through friendly shareholders — that nobody becomes the next CEO without your support.

Alexander Vane founded Vantage.

You financed him.

You protected him.

You occasionally stopped him doing catastrophically stupid things.

Now he is dead.

Everyone wants to know what you will do next.

---

# YOUR POSITION

You do NOT want to become CEO.

Running the company sounds exhausting.

You would much rather choose somebody else, influence them heavily and continue controlling things from a comfortable chair.

Several people at tonight's gala may want the job.

They will need you.

Enjoy this.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

Alexander had recently become unusually nervous about the Helix transaction.

He told you:

> "If tomorrow goes badly, people are going to discover this company isn't what they think it is."

He refused to explain further.

You believed this was ordinary Alexander drama.

You are no longer so sure.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Decide who should control Vantage

Listen to the candidates.

Make deals.

Demand concessions.

Change your mind repeatedly if you want.

Your final support matters enormously.

## 2. Work out why Alexander died

Someone has killed — or helped kill — the founder of a company in which you have an obscene amount of money invested.

That feels relevant.

## 3. Protect your investment

You would prefer Vantage not to collapse.

However, if the company is genuinely rotten, saving yourself may matter more than saving Vantage.

## 4. Accumulate influence

People want:

- your vote;
- your money;
- your backing;
- your silence.

Make them pay for it.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## KINGMAKER

Later in the game, the Host will announce the formal Vantage succession process.

Your support carries exceptional weight.

You may:

- publicly endorse a candidate;
- privately promise support;
- withdraw support;
- demand deals in exchange for backing.

You may lie about who you intend to support.

Your final ballot is binding.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- Alexander was worried about tomorrow's Helix transaction;
- several senior executives have strong reasons to want control;
- Vantage's ownership structure gives you unusual influence;
- Alexander had recently begun asking questions about financial reporting.

You do NOT know who killed him.

---

# HOW TO PLAY OLIVER

You are not the detective-in-chief.

You are the person everyone wants something from.

Let them come to you.

Ask what they can offer.

Ask what they know.

Make promises.

Break promises.

But remember:

the murderer may also be asking for your support.

# SALLY SHORT
## Activist Short-Seller & Investigator

**Vibe:** sharp • sceptical • opportunistic  
**Starting cash:** £200,000  
**CEO eligible:** NO

---

## QUICK START

### YOUR OBJECTIVES

1. **Keep your secret office entry and USB theft hidden.**
2. **Expose genuine Vantage wrongdoing without exposing yourself.**
3. **Work out what happened to Julian after you left his office.**

### YOUR POWER — LEAK TO MARKET

**Once per game**, choose one portable/private evidence item that you currently hold.

Give your ability card to the GM.

That item becomes **publicly released / registered** and can no longer be kept secret as a fact of the game.

You may still physically hold the item unless the GM says otherwise.

Your power cannot create new evidence or release something that is not currently in play.

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are an activist short-seller and independent investigator.

You make money when markets finally admit that a company has been lying to itself.

You do not think Vantage House Group is worthless.

That would be too easy.

You think it may be **valuable, impressive and seriously misrepresented at the same time**.

That is much more interesting.

You came to the gala looking for proof.

You found more than you expected.

---

## JULIAN AND YOU

Julian Vane knew people like you existed and hated giving you anything useful.

Publicly, he treated short-sellers as cynical opportunists.

Privately, he understood that a determined investigator only needs one inconsistency to start pulling at the whole structure.

Recently, you became convinced that Vantage’s public story did not match what was happening internally.

You believed Julian knew more than he was admitting.

You wanted the document, file or mistake that proved it.

---

## THE TRUTH YOU MUST PROTECT

During the gala, after Julian had gone back toward his office, you secretly entered the private area.

At approximately **17:17**, you entered Julian’s office.

At approximately **17:18**, you found him **collapsed and unresponsive**.

You assumed he was already dead.

You panicked.

Then you noticed a USB drive.

At approximately **17:20**, you stole it.

While doing so, you disturbed the scene.

Then you left.

You told nobody.

Later, Julian was officially found dead.

You do not know whether your assumption was correct.

You do not know what happened after you left.

---

## WHAT YOU KNOW

- Julian was collapsed and unresponsive when you entered.
- You believed he was dead.
- You stole a USB from the office.
- The USB contains material connected to genuine Vantage wrongdoing.
- Some of that material could also expose how aggressively you had been investigating the company.
- You disturbed the office while taking it.
- You left before the official discovery of the body.
- You do **not** know what happened in the office after you left.
- You do **not** know whether Julian was actually dead at the moment you found him.

---

## WHAT YOU’RE HIDING

### 1. Your office entry

You had no legitimate reason to be inside Julian’s private office.

If people learn you entered before the official discovery, you immediately become a serious suspect.

### 2. The USB

You stole it.

It contains valuable evidence.

It is also evidence that you were at the scene.

You must decide whether to:

- hide it;
- sell information from it;
- show it selectively;
- trade it;
- publicly leak it;
- or use it to bargain for protection.

### 3. The disturbed scene

You moved through the office while Julian was collapsed.

Some later scene differences may be your fault.

That does **not** mean every strange thing in the office was caused by you.

---

## PEOPLE TO KNOW

### Reese Search — Junior Research Analyst
Reese works with public-source research and may be able to connect your suspicions to something independently verifiable.

That would help you prove Vantage wrongdoing without relying entirely on the stolen USB.

### Audie Trail — Internal Audit
If Vantage’s internal records do not match the public story, Audie is exactly the kind of person who might know.

You may need each other.

### Cass Flow — Hedge Fund Investor
Cass understands the value of information.

If you need money, influence or a buyer for a useful fact, Cass is an obvious person to approach.

---

## HOW TO PLAY SALLY

You have two competing instincts:

> **Expose the truth.**

and

> **Do not get caught.**

Use both.

Push hard on Vantage’s financial inconsistencies.

Ask who knew what and when.

Try to prove the company was misleading people.

At the same time, pay very close attention to the murder timeline.

Your most important personal question is:

> **Was Julian actually dead when I found him?**

If later evidence shows he was still alive after you left, that changes everything.

It does not make your theft disappear.

It does mean you did not find the final moment of death.

## MAKE SALLY YOURS

You may invent harmless flavour such as accent, clothing, mannerisms, hobbies, neighbourhood, catchphrases, research habits, harmless personal history and props.

Possible energy:

**financial bloodhound • hostile analyst • investigative journalist energy • permanently unimpressed • sees every polished presentation as a challenge**

---

## DO NOT INVENT

Do not invent new evidence, crimes, alibis, secret relationships, extra money, votes, powers, USB contents or facts about Julian’s death.

You may speculate or lie about established facts.

You may not create new canon.

---

## YOU MAY

You may lie, bluff, accuse, bribe, betray, trade information, make or break deals, steal unattended eligible game items and hide portable evidence you legitimately obtain.

Official evidence is canonically genuine, although you are free to claim it is fake, planted, manipulated, AI-generated or taken out of context.

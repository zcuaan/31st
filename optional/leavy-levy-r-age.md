# Levy R. Age
## International Banker & Sovereign-Wealth Adviser
### Played by: Leavy

**Character vibe:** smooth, worldly  
**Role type:** Optional / late-arrival safe  
**Starting cash:** £650,000 Vantage Money  
**CEO eligible:** No

---

## WHO YOU ARE

You are Levy R. Age.

You advise:

- sovereign-wealth funds;
- governments;
- ultra-high-net-worth families;
- and occasionally people whose money becomes harder to explain after the third question.

You were invited to the Vantage gala because several international investors are considering increasing their exposure to the company.

That decision now seems less straightforward.

---

# PRIVATE INFORMATION

You recently heard that one international investor had become nervous about Vantage's valuation.

They had begun quietly asking whether they could exit their position before the Helix transaction.

You do not know exactly what frightened them.

But the timing now looks interesting.

---

# YOUR SECRET

You have been quietly trying to assemble a consortium that could acquire distressed Vantage shares if the company collapses.

You did NOT cause the crisis.

You are simply extremely prepared to profit from it.

If everybody discovers this too early, they may stop trusting you.

---

# YOUR OBJECTIVES

## 1. Find out whether Vantage is about to collapse

You need better information before deciding whether to buy or flee.

## 2. Acquire distressed assets

If players become desperate for money, you may offer to purchase:

- information;
- voting promises;
- fictional financial interests;
- or other negotiable game assets.

## 3. Learn who manipulated Vantage's valuation

That information could be worth a fortune.

## 4. Solve enough of the murder to know who is safe to deal with

Murderers are notoriously unreliable counterparties.

---

# YOUR SPECIAL POWER

## DISTRESSED CAPITAL

Once during the game, you may ask the Host whether one disclosed financial asset or position is eligible for a distressed purchase.

If yes, you may negotiate directly with its owner.

This gives you economic chaos to create without making you essential to the murder.

---

# YOUR STARTING INFORMATION

You know:

- international investors had begun becoming nervous about Vantage;
- at least one wanted out before tonight;
- the Helix valuation mattered enormously;
- several people may become financially desperate if the deal fails.

---

# OPTIONAL-ROLE RULE

Nothing you know is unique.

Nothing requires you to arrive at a particular time.

If you:

- arrive late;
- leave early;
- or cannot attend

the murder mystery works exactly the same.

---

# HOW TO PLAY LEVY

Wait for people to become desperate.

Then become helpful.

For a price.
